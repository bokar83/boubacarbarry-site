The Voice Engine

A founder gets five things in a fixed order, over 30 days · updated 2026-08-19

The short version

Five pillars, in sequence, and each one is what makes the next possible. A recorded working session, a written voice guide built from it, four finished pieces published in your voice, a prompt pack your team can run, and a one-page weekly process for keeping it going.

What you own at the end: your voice written down, four pieces live, and a repeatable way to keep publishing without hiring anyone.

The stack, in the order it happens

The bold line is the one-sentence version. The line under it is the detail, for anyone who wants the step by step.

1. One recorded working session, 60 minutes.

I ask, you talk. That hour is the only time I need from you.

2. A written voice guide, built from that session.

How you actually think and talk, on paper. Your positions, your phrasing, the things you refuse to say.

3. Four finished pieces, published in your voice.

The guide is what makes them sound like you and not like a ghostwriter. Two rounds of revisions on each.

4. A prompt pack your own team can run.

The same instructions I used, handed to you, so the next piece does not wait on me.

5. A one-page weekly process.

The rhythm that keeps it going after I step out. Something you run in a half hour a week.

What you own at the end: your voice written down, four pieces live, and a repeatable way to keep publishing without hiring anyone. That is the part that stays.

How it starts, the term, and what is not included

How it starts. I write and deliver the first piece before any money moves. You read it, then you decide.

Term. 30 days from the working session, then month to month with 30 days notice. Nothing locks in.

Not included. I do not run your accounts and I do not write replies as you. No design, no video, no ads, and no distribution beyond where you already post. Revisions cap at two rounds per piece.

Capacity. Three founders at a time, and that is a real number, not a tactic.

Price

I name the numbers on the call. A fixed price for the first 30 days, and a monthly price if you want me to keep going.

Your outreach engine doc says that once you run the engine for yourself you can run it for a client, and that it is a bigger and stickier offer than writing posts. Should this be the whole engine instead of the pieces? That is the read I want from you.
