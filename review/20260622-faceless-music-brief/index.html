Faceless Music Channel
Sonic Brief + Council Analysis

Two channels evaluated. One brief ready to launch. One needs narrowing. Council ran both. This doc captures the full analysis so the decision is durable — not buried in a chat transcript.

Why We Are Doing This

The goal is a faceless YouTube channel that generates revenue autonomously — no on-camera presence required. Music is the right entry point because it does not require a face, a script, or an avatar session to start. The music IS the content.

Two channel concepts were evaluated side by side: a high-energy affluent EDM channel and a West African neural beats focus channel. Both are faceless. The question was which one to launch first and why.

The answer came down to one thing: defensibility. Anyone can launch a deep house channel. Nobody but you can launch the West African neural beats channel. Guinea is not a hat you put on. It is where you are from.

Competitor Research — Both Niches

Every top channel in both niches runs without an avatar. Logo, visualizer, or ambient visuals only. The music carries the channel. This confirms: no avatar needed to start.

Brief 1 — Energy Channel (Affluent EDM)

NOT READY — Needs Narrowing

"This channel sounds like a rooftop office at midnight with the city below — deep house, progressive techno, and high-energy dance that feels expensive, never cheap. Every track has driving momentum, minimal lyrics, deep basslines, crisp percussion, anthemic builds. The listener feels unstoppable and elevated, moving at a higher frequency than everyone else."

Council — 5 Voices

The Contrarian

Three genres in one brief is three channels, not one. Deep house, progressive techno, and high-energy dance do not sound the same. The brief names a feeling, not a sound. Pick one genre or the channel has no sonic identity to anchor on.

The First Principles Thinker

The listener is blurry. "Unstoppable and elevated" fits someone working out AND someone building at 2am AND someone in a club. One person, one moment. Lock that down before generating a track.

The Expansionist

"Affluent twist" is the entire differentiator and it is not defined in sonic terms. What does expensive sound like vs cheap EDM? Orchestral layers? Slower BPM? No synth distortion on the drop? Name the sonic detail that carries "affluent" — or the brief is a mood board, not a brief.

The Outsider

A stranger clicking this channel does not know what to expect before the music plays. That is the brief's job — to make the sound predictable so the audience self-selects. Right now it does not do that job.

The Executor

Rewrite to one sonic identity: "Deep house with orchestral builds. 120–126 BPM. Energy through momentum and layering, not explosive drops. No aggressive synth distortion." Generate 3 tracks from that rewrite. If all three feel expensive and unstoppable, the brief is ready. If they don't, the rewrite was wrong — adjust and test again.

Brief 2 — Focus Channel (West African Neural Beats) LAUNCH THIS FIRST

READY WITH ONE TEST

"This channel sounds like studying while something ancient and alive hums underneath — kora and balafon woven into binaural beats and ambient electronic. Every track has precisely tuned neural frequencies (40Hz gamma for active focus, 10Hz alpha for calm concentration) layered under West African instrumentation. The listener feels rooted, sharp, and focused beyond distraction — as if the music knows where they come from and where they are going."

Council — 5 Voices

The Contrarian

Has anyone tested whether kora and balafon work WITH binaural frequencies? Kora is microtonal. Binaural beats are precision-frequency. They might fight each other rather than stack. This needs one test track before the brief is locked. The concept is right. The instrumentation pairing is unproven.

The First Principles Thinker

The moat is airtight. This is the only channel that combines West African instruments with scientifically-tuned focus frequencies. Cultural authority cannot be faked — it requires being from the culture, not just sampling it. No competitor can replicate this specific positioning.

The Expansionist

This brief opens a category, not just a channel. "The sounds your ancestors used for focus, optimized with modern neuroscience." That is an African cognitive science brand. In 12 months this is not a YouTube channel — it is a label, a curriculum, a product line. The brief undersells where this goes.

The Outsider

"40Hz gamma, 10Hz alpha" will not land with most listeners. Lead with the emotional promise — "rooted, sharp, focused beyond distraction" — and use the frequency science as proof in the description, not as the headline. The cultural depth is the hook. The science is the credibility layer.

The Executor

Generate one test track on Suno this week: kora + ambient electronic, 10Hz alpha layer underneath, 60–70 BPM. Listen to it. Does the kora mesh with the binaural frequencies or does it fight them? If it meshes — launch with this brief as written. If it clashes — switch primary instrument to balafon (percussion pairs better with binaural layers). Report back before generating the remaining tracks.

The Francophone Angle — Why This Matters Long-Term

The study/focus niche has 9+ million subscribers across the top 3 channels and none of them carry a West African or Francophone identity. The audience for focus music is global. The Francophone African diaspora alone is 300+ million people. A channel that speaks to that identity — through sound, not language — has a cultural gravity no synthetic competitor can replicate.

Review this angle in 60 days: if the first 10 tracks confirm the kora/binaural pairing works, the expansion play is a Francophone-specific channel companion — same neural beats, with French-language video titles and descriptions targeting diaspora search terms. Two channels, one production pipeline, two separate audiences.

The energy channel (Brief 1) is also worth building — but after the brief is narrowed and after Focus proves the production workflow. Do not run both simultaneously until Focus has 10 tracks posted and a baseline retention curve.
