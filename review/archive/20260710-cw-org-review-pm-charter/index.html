3 · The PM Crew Charter FULL PURVIEW ▸
Mission. Be the chief-of-staff layer for the whole org: keep every content bucket from running dry, never let a meeting arrive un-prepped, track program-KPI pace so paid coaching pays back, and detect cross-lane slips (has-slipped and about-to-slip) before they cost revenue. One board, one morning view. Read-only externally in v1, no auto-send.

Purview (includes items Boubacar did NOT list)

  • Content-bucket runway management. LinkedIn, X, newsletter, IG, TikTok, YouTube, Blotato. Track queue depth vs cadence, compute a "runs dry" date per bucket, and flag any bucket with fewer than N days of buffer so refills are scheduled days ahead. Not listed but critical: the 5 warmup-gated faceless channels (graduation is a Boubacar-only flag), the studio "personal" rows that silently hard-skip, and the Content Board backlog of written-but-never-fired drafts.
  • Meeting + podcast + outreach tracking with prep-deadline reminders. Prep-today-for-tomorrow alerts (e.g. Nabrotzky prep due 7/13 for a 7/14 meeting). Not listed: duplicate/reschedule detection (the Nabrotzky 7/14 vs 7/17 dup), calendar-rule violations (Hirz.ai 6am Sunday breaks the never-before-8:30 rule), the podcast pipeline gated on an unbuilt conversion-fork page, and warm-contact follow-up state (the 4 non-Nabrotzky reconnects with no tracked status).
  • IC + PGA program obligations + KPI pace (ROI on paid programs). Weekly call obligations, assignment track, the recurring recap owed to Devin, and pace vs targets (discovery calls, posts, connection requests, DMs). Not listed: a real vs designed catch-up rate so an idle day visibly raises tomorrow's quota, and a "which coach grades what first" note so effort matches the graded metric.
  • Cross-lane slip detection (has-slipped + could-slip). Watch cron liveness ledgers, last-post timestamps, and queue depths across every lane; surface silent stops. Not listed: watchdog-of-the-watchdog (cron-health-check itself was off for 3+ weeks and nothing noticed), and a "stale payload" check (the lighthouse digest fires on time but re-sends a May 16 file).
  • Revenue + conversion tracking. Not listed but the whole point: a dollar counter vs the $3K-by-Sep-20 goal, and reply/call/deal conversions, not just asks-sent. Today the Money Map counts sends but nothing downstream.
  • Money-path integrity. Not listed: proactively verify that every "live" checkout still resolves (dead-pay-link class), that promo posts actually fired (the 4 FB workshop posts did not), and that success pages return to our own upsell.
  • Daily morning scorecard ritual. One assembled view (section 4) plus a short "what changed since yesterday" diff.
