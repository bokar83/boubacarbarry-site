1. Results email v2

Subject: Your [city] dentist scan, [name]

Hi [name], You asked to see it, so here it is. No pitch wrapped around it. I asked ChatGPT, Gemini, and Perplexity who they would recommend for a dentist in [city]. I ran [queries_run] versions of that question. [practice] came up [times_named]. The names that came up most instead were [competitor_1] and [competitor_2]. Here is why that matters in plain terms. More patients are skipping the Google map and just asking AI "who is the best dentist near me." Every time that happens in [city] right now, the answer points at [competitor_1] or [competitor_2], and that patient books with them. Not because they are better dentists. Because the AI trusts what it can read about them more than what it can read about you. Two things usually cause that, and both are fixable: 1. Your own site does not say, in plain words, "dentist in [city]" and list what you actually do. AI reads that first, before it ever looks at your reviews. 2. The places AI already trusts (your Google Business Profile, the directories that list you) do not line up with each other. When the details disagree, AI plays it safe and names someone else. I cannot tell from the outside which one is the bigger lever for you. Could be one, could be both, could be something I have not named yet. That is the part worth ten minutes. If you grab a slot here, I will walk you through the full scan, show you the single highest-leverage line your site is missing, and you will leave the call with that one fix written out whether or not we ever work together: [calendly] Or just reply and I will send the breakdown in writing. Whichever is easier. Either way, now you know what your patients are being told. Boubacar

★ The highest-leverage missing piece (not copy)

The offer can't close without ONE real before/after. Take one practice (friend / referral / honest pilot), run scan → setup → re-scan in 30 days, screenshot both ("named [low] → named [higher]"). That one documented movement becomes the proof line in every future email + meeting. Worth more than any subject test. It does not exist yet — build it first.

Warm-first (before scaling cold): quiet past Calendly bookers, referrals, dentists in network → work these first. Warm converts higher AND is the most likely source of the pilot case that becomes your cold proof. Two birds.
