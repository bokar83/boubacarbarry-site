The real problem (why cold-to-$47 failed, in one equation)

The 0-paid result was not a copy problem. It is a value-equation problem. Hormozi: Value = (Dream Outcome × Perceived Likelihood) / (Time Delay × Effort & Sacrifice). A cold stranger sent straight to a $47 in-person weeknight local event faces the worst possible denominator:

LeverCold → $47 in-person (current)Score
Dream outcome"Use AI without getting burned" — decent, but generic to a stranger6/10
Perceived likelihoodWho is this guy? Will a Tuesday night in Sandy be worth it? No proof they trust yet3/10
Time delayEvent is 2+ weeks out AND they must show up on one fixed evening3/10
Effort & sacrificeDrive to Sandy, give up a weeknight 7-8:30, $47 up front, block the calendar, show up alone to a stranger's event2/10

Average ~3.5/10. A hard fail. The bottom of the equation is brutal and cold paid cannot fix it. Hormozi's rule: the denominator is the cheat code. So the whole strategy is to attack time delay and effort:

  • Lower the first ask to free (the audit) → kills the $47 up-front friction and gives an instant win (time delay → seconds).
  • Warm the audience before the ask → fixes perceived likelihood (they know you, or a peer vouched).
  • Remove "showing up alone" → bring-a-peer offer kills a real in-person friction.

Second diagnosis: you skipped the activation order. Hormozi's order is Warm → Content → Cold outreach → Paid. The spend went straight to Paid (channel 4) while Warm (1), Content (2), and Cold outreach (3) are barely worked. Paid accelerates a broken funnel. For a 17-day local fill, the fastest seats are in channels 1-2, which cost almost nothing.
