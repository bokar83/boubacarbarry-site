6. Stop accepting introductions that position you incorrectly

This is the only step no agent can build for you. What an agent can do is name exactly what a bad introduction looks like in your case, so you recognise it in the second it happens instead of three days later.

A bad introduction, for you, is any of these

  • “Boubacar spent ten years in HR at a Fortune 100 company.” True, complete, and it ends the sentence on the old you. It is the most common one and it usually comes from somebody being kind.
  • “He is looking for an HR Director role.” True this month. It closes the door on the fractional and advisory conversation in the same breath.
  • “He knows a lot about AI.” Generic, and it files you with ten thousand other people.
  • Any introduction where your name arrives attached to a job title rather than to a problem you solve.
