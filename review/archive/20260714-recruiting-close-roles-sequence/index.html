Core reframe: competitive to complementary. The copy never says "I close roles faster" (arrogant to a career recruiter). It offers the one thing they can't see: the buyer side of their own deals.

  • Condescension fixed: opens on their pain and their expertise, not a lecture.
  • Deliverability: plain-text, no links/pixels; SPF/DKIM/DMARC confirmed; stagger sends.

  • Origin role inbox: capped to T1 + T3.
