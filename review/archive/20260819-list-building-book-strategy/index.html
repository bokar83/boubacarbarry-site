Three deliberate departures from the teardown

  1. Free before paid, in that order. He could open with a paid tripwire because he had 440,000 people to filter. At 32, a $9 price filters out the list before it exists. The rule this build most often fails, and it already failed once on your own site

Every email captured must land in the real store and be read back out by hand before the page is called done. A green form submission is not proof, and a thank-you screen is definitely not proof, which is exactly how the boubacarbarry.com form has been telling people they subscribed. The capture also has to sit in front of every off-page handoff, not merely on the same page as one, because a destination in a link is one click away and nothing gates it. Check both on the day it goes live.
