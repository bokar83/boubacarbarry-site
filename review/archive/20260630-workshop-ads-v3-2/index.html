Workshop ads v3.2 — the promise now matches the room

Beginner in-person AI workshop · Thu July 30 · Sandy, UT · $47 (reg $97). Same seminar photos, same bright type. The promise is re-grounded to what the workshop actually delivers.

What changed and why. The earlier version implied a done-with-you build ("bring your task, leave with AI doing it for you"). That is not what happens in the room. The workshop teaches three simple AI tools owners use themselves. v3.2 says exactly that, and no more. Underpromise, then overdeliver. Then it makes the honest promise concrete enough to act on with an owner-payoff line, so smaller-but-true does not read as small.

  • "Bring one real task. Leave with AI doing it for you."

  • Sells only what is delivered: three simple tools the attendee uses themselves.

Pre-mortem (buyer POV) and how each kill-reason was closed

Frame: launch day, ads ran, zero registered. Autopsy from the skeptical Utah owner's chair. The one lens for everything: would this make a real owner click "Save your seat"?

  • "3 AI tools reads as free / Google-able." Closed: the owner-payoff subhead turns "tools" into outcomes (answers, faster calls, catch costly mistakes) so the value is in the result, not the tool count.
  • B's soft "where do you start?" hook had no stakes. Closed: rewritten to the pain-hook "You keep hearing AI will help. Nobody hands you the how." Same tension structure as the lead.
  • "Start with 3 tools" leaked an upsell feel. Closed: changed to "Leave with," restoring same-day self-sufficiency.
  • Underpromising could read as underwhelming. Closed: the promise stays true but is made vivid with three concrete owner payoffs, not inflated back into a done-with-you claim.
  • Unknown host, no proof. Held honestly: credibility rests on the true practitioner line plus "a workshop, not a pitch." No fabricated numbers, testimonials, or ROI claims were added (truth-only).

Two integrity notes carried forward (not fixed in-creative, by design): the tools are described in plain function only, never named on the ad (names live on the landing page). Scarcity stays "small group, limited seats" with no invented count. The $47 (reg $97) framing is the existing live seat-page pricing.
