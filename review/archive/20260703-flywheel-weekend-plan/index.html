The specifics behind the ranking (current 2026 tactics)

LinkedIn (his primary)

  • Dwell time is the top signal — a 15-second read earns an est. 40% reach bonus. Saves are the strongest quality signal.
  • Post 2–3 researched posts/week, not more (over-posting cannibalizes reach). 800–1500 chars. Specific lessons, real stories, sharp frameworks.
  • Topic authority: stay on a few connected subjects (AI-readiness, HR, building in the open) so the algo learns to route him.
  • Native video viewership up 36% YoY and growing 2x faster than any format — use it.
  • Comments of 5+ words count; spend saved time on substantive engagement, 15 min/day.

Short-form video (B2B)

  • First 3 seconds is the whole hook — earn attention immediately or lose it.
  • B2B short-form is a top-ROI investment now because adoption is still low = unusually high organic reach.
  • Lengths: TikTok 15–30s (rewards completion), Reels 30–60s, Shorts 60–90s, LinkedIn native (early = high reach).
  • AI tooling makes volume realistic — but for HIM, reuse ONE recording, don't manufacture 20/week.

GEO (his own product, dogfooded)

  • Search volume projected -25% by 2026; ~51% of software buyers now start research with an AI chatbot.
  • Citation lift: quotations +41%, statistics +32%, citations +30%, fluency +28%.
  • Tactics: TL;DR summaries, clear claims + evidence, Q&A sections, data tables, visible author + date. Results in 4–8 weeks.
  • Double win: being cited proves the exact thing he charges $47/$250 to fix.

Borrowed audiences + repurposing

  • Newsletter swaps + small aligned podcasts build trust faster than cold promo. Pick partners who say yes this week, not prestige names with slow cycles.
  • A 3,000-listener aligned show beats a 50,000 generic one for qualified leads. Feed swaps can convert 10–40x a promo read.
  • COPE: one video → 12–40 assets. Workflow: intake → edit → approve → schedule. Each piece native to its platform, never copy-paste.

5. Council pass — kill the "feels productive, produces nothing" failure

1. Contrarian

The named trap: a weekend of "content" that generates views, not buyers. Guardrail — every play must point at ONE live checkout, and success is measured in clicks-to-scan + sales, not follower counts. Fresh-Facebook posting and generic Reddit spray are cut as vanity.

2. First-principles

Revenue = traffic × conversion × price. Conversion + price already exist on every asset; only traffic is missing. So 100% of effort goes to traffic — and to his warmest, highest-fit traffic first (LinkedIn), not the coldest (fresh faceless reach) that feels busy.

3. Finance

His cheapest, fastest traffic is an audience he already has (LinkedIn) plus borrowed audiences (swaps/podcasts). Near-zero marginal cost, real ROI. Paid into an unproven funnel is the one negative-EV move — frozen.

4. Brand

Pointing his real audience at a genuinely useful free tool builds the "operator who ships" moat and the building-in-the-open theme. Never imply AI writes his posts — his voice is 100% his; the system only schedules. Landmines fenced: the "$0-to-first-client" product, a thin template.

5. Executor

Solo, two holiday days. His time is the scarce input, so his list is capped at record-2 + approve-4 + pitch-3 + comment-daily. The system absorbs the rest. Saturday's engine before Sunday's nice-to-haves.

Honest verdict

Moves the needle this weekend: LinkedIn posts/video to his warm audience + the atomization rail + switching the $47 tool live. Moves it in 30 days: borrowed audiences + GEO citation + faceless compounding. Vanity to skip: fresh Facebook, follower-chasing, un-targeted Reddit, any ad spend now.
