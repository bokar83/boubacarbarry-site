0TL;DR — the one insight per lane

Cross-cutting wedge (biggest finding): the word "actually" is the dominant qualifier in every lane, on both Google and Reddit — "AI tools that actually work", "how businesses actually use AI", "AI recruiting that actually helps", "cut hiring costs at scale". Buyers are past the hype and openly skeptical (Reddit: "is AI consulting a scam", "AAA feel like a scam", "pure bluff", "the AI consulting gold rush"). The buyer is not asking "what is AI" anymore. They are asking "who will actually make it work on MY business, for real." That is the exact seam a truth-only operator who builds in the open owns. Every retrofit below runs through it.
  • Local workshop: the money is in local + "near me" + "in person" language tied to Utah. Real strings people type: "ai classes near salt lake city", "ai classes near me for adults", "in person ai training", "ai training for small business owners". The current page sells the emotion ("nobody shows you how") but is likely invisible to the literal search string. Inject the geo + "in person" + "for small business owners" terms.
  • Fractional / consultant: "fractional AI" as a bare term is a trap — its search results are polluted by aircraft ownership, pool-aiming, job-seekers, and a company Anthropic literally acquired named "Fractional AI". Buyers instead search "AI consultant for small business", "AI consulting for small businesses", "boutique AI consulting", and the emerging "fractional Chief AI Officer". Spell out the function; do not lean on the raw phrase.
  • Recruiting / staffing: lead with "time to fill" and "cut hiring costs" — these are the metric words firm owners actually search, and they map 1:1 to the HR seat. Winning frame everywhere: "AI won't replace recruiters, the recruiters who use it will win." Avoid "automate recruiting" as a literal term (polluted by a college-football video game).

The 5 corrections that shaped this report▼
  • Intent contamination is the #1 risk. A large share of "AI consultant / AI agency / learn AI / AI course" volume is job-seeker or agency-builder intent, not buyer intent. Every term is tagged; the builder/job terms are flagged to exclude from paid targeting. Chasing raw volume here would burn budget on people who will never buy.
  • Two terms killed as literal keywords: "fractional AI" (polluted by aircraft/pool/job-seekers + the acquired company) and "automate recruiting" (a college-football video game dominates). Both survive only as spelled-out functions, never as the raw search target.
  • "Free" is a filter, not a target. It is the most common Lane-1 modifier. Targeting it attracts non-buyers. Aim at "in person / for small business owners / worth it" instead.
  • The "actually / proof / anti-scam" wedge is real leverage, not a cute pattern. It recurred independently across Google, YouTube, and Reddit in all three lanes, and it maps precisely to the truth-only, build-in-the-open, credentialed-operator position. Elevated to the through-line.
  • Truthfulness guard on the retrofit. Excluded every hype number that showed up in Google PAA ("$900k AI job", "$1000/day", "85% of projects fail"). Kept the copy first-person, no em-dashes, 10-year GE / Fortune 100, no fabricated results.
