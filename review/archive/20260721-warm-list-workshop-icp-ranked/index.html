Methodology & what wasn't checked

  • Base data: the master scored file (warm-list-ranked-2026-06-30.md) covers 4,346 real LinkedIn connections on a 6-signal weighted rubric. That file prints the full 63-row Workshop A-tier, a top-~60 sample of the 1,248-row Workshop B-tier, the full Audit A/B tables (13 rows), and the full Ghostwriting A-tier (16 rows). This review re-rates all 152 of those printed rows. It does not re-score the remaining ~1,188 B-tier or ~6,000 C-tier rows that were never printed anywhere -- those only exist in the original LinkedIn export / Supabase, which this pass did not query.
  • Two new dimensions applied on top of the original score: (1) workshop-attendee fit -- Utah geo plus a real owner/operator/leader role inside a business or institution with employees, never a solopreneur or pure individual contributor; (2) referral value -- explicit evidence that Boubacar himself flagged the person as a connector, not an assumption. Where the data gave no referral evidence, the row was never placed in bucket B on a guess -- it went to C or D instead.
  • Hard exclusions applied everywhere: coaches and accountability partners and personal relationships (Brandon Orton, Rachel Weiss), AI consultants/agencies as direct competitors (Luke Pierce, Brendan Langen), and solopreneurs with no visible team (fractional CHROs/consultants in the audit A-tier).
  • Geo corrections applied from Boubacar's own 2026-07-02 notes: Cheikh Ndiaye is in Georgia, not Utah (moved to bucket D for this workshop). Lindy Mansfield is Utah-confirmed, not Unknown (moved to bucket A).
  • Geo conflicts found by reading the company field against the geo tag (not fabricated, cross-checked against the text already in the data): Mariana Monteiro and Janice L Semper are tagged "Utah" but their employers are Connecticut state/nonprofit entities; Mark Homer's employer is BYU-Idaho (Rexburg, ID), not Utah; Rachel Weiss's employer, Helen of Troy, is HQ'd in Texas. All four moved out of bucket A.
  • Two soft geo flags, not corrections -- kept in bucket C because the evidence is inconclusive, not because it's wrong: Tom Whitworth (Wasatch Photonics -- name suggests Utah but HQ location could not be confirmed from this data) and SETH OGOE AYIM (a "(Global)" chapter title whose home base is unclear).
  • Nothing in this file was fabricated. Every name, company, title, and score traces to the source files named above. Rows where fit or geo could not be determined were placed in C or D and marked accordingly -- never guessed into A.
  • Not verified in this pass: current employment status (some of these LinkedIn snapshots may be stale), whether "Utah" rows are still physically local today, and any referral ties beyond what Boubacar had already noted in prior files. A fast human skim of bucket C (10 rows) is the highest-value next step.
