Why this role exists right now

The separation: NBCUniversal is becoming its own company ›

Fact On June 29, 2026, Comcast announced a plan to split into two independent public companies. It will spin off NBCUniversal and Sky into a separate media and entertainment company, while Comcast keeps broadband, wireless and cable. The new NBCUniversal carries Universal Pictures, the NBC and Telemundo broadcast networks, NBC News, Peacock, Bravo, the theme parks, and Sky.

Fact Leadership is set: current Comcast co-CEO Mike Cavanagh becomes CEO of the standalone NBCUniversal. Former Comcast CFO Michael Angelakis becomes CEO of Comcast after the split. The separation is structured as a tax-free spinoff and is expected to complete in roughly a year, so through 2026 into 2027 NBCU is actively building itself into an independent company.

Sources: CNBC, NBC News, The Hollywood Reporter, CBS News.
Versant: they already ran one spinoff this year ›

Fact The Versant Media Group spinoff, which carved most of NBCU's cable networks out into a separate company (USA, CNBC, MSNBC, Oxygen, E!, SYFY, Golf Channel, plus Fandango and Rotten Tomatoes), completed effective January 2, 2026, and began trading on Nasdaq as VSNT on January 5, 2026. The stock fell more than 14% on its debut.

Inference A company that just untangled cable, and is now untangling the entire media business from its cable/broadband parent, is doing an enormous amount of org design, workforce re-mapping, and reporting-line rework. That is HR transformation work by definition, and it explains why the role names workforce planning and org design as required.

Sources: CNBC, Variety, Forbes.
What this likely means for the hiring manager (labeled inference) ›

Inference "People Strategy and HR Transformation" inside Operations and Technology, during a separation, most likely means: design the people operating model and governance for a company that is becoming independent, while the HR technology stack is being rebuilt underneath it. The hiring manager's fear is not a lack of ideas. It is execution risk, transformation pilots that never become anything, and governance that does not hold up once NBCU has its own board, its own balance sheet, and its own auditors.

Inference That is why the description pairs "transformation roadmap" with "governance and performance metrics" and "evaluate emerging technologies including AI-enabled HR capabilities." They want someone who can sequence the change and prove it, not just recommend tools.

NBCU's AI posture

They are building AI as an internal capability, not just buying it ›

Fact NBCU is hiring into a dedicated internal AI function inside the same Operations and Technology org that is hiring you. Open and recent roles include a Director, AI Strategy & Innovation, a Senior Data Governance Engineer (AI), and a Senior Technical Product Owner for Prism Services (AI Solutions) building enterprise generative-AI agents for conversational and analytics use cases.

Fact Through Comcast NBCUniversal LIFT Labs, the company runs an accelerator that pairs startups directly with Comcast, NBCUniversal, and Sky teams. A prior Generative AI Accelerator cohort saw every participating startup secure a pilot or proof of concept with one of the three, and the Fall 2025 cohort was organized around themes including AI governance and data enablement.

Inference This matters for your posture. NBCU is not AI-curious and looking for a believer. They already have AI builders. What they lack in the HR seat is someone who has actually operated an AI system in production and can bring that operator judgment to the people function. That is a narrower, rarer profile, and it is yours.

Sources: Built In NYC (Director, AI Strategy & Innovation), Built In NYC (Sr Data Governance Engineer, AI), Built In (Sr Technical Product Owner, AI Solutions), LIFT Labs, Comcast Corporate.
Parent-company context: Comcast's broader AI bet ›

Fact / third-party analysis A published analyst review of Comcast's AI strategy describes "Project Janus," an initiative to virtualize Comcast's core network with AI-driven self-healing and automatic rerouting. This is a Comcast network/broadband initiative, not an NBCU media or HR program, so treat it as backdrop: it signals the parent has been investing in AI-at-scale and operational automation for years.

Inference Do not lead with Janus on the call. It is parent-company network engineering, not your lane. It is useful only as a one-line signal that AI-driven operations are already part of the corporate DNA, so an HR transformation that leans on AI will not feel foreign to leadership.

Source: Klover.ai analyst review (third-party, secondary source, not a Comcast/NBCU statement).

AI governance, the part the role names explicitly

What is publicly visible on their governance stance ›

Fact NBCU has hired for AI governance directly. The Senior Data Governance Engineer (AI) role is described as designing and implementing governance guardrails for AI models and agents in partnership with NBCU Legal, Privacy, and Cyber teams, including monitoring and reporting frameworks and usage tracking. So "governance" in the job description is not abstract, they are actively building the muscle.

Fact LIFT Labs explicitly sought AI-governance startups in its Fall 2025 cohort, including tools for IP protection against generative-AI misuse and agentic data-governance platforms. Governance is a stated priority theme, not an afterthought.

Fact At the parent level, shareholders have filed proposals pushing Comcast for greater transparency on its use of AI and ethical guidelines, referencing an AI Bill of Rights framing. Investor pressure on responsible AI is real and on the record.

Sources: Built In NYC (Sr Data Governance Engineer, AI), LIFT Labs, Interfaith Center on Corporate Responsibility, PRI.
The honest gap in the public record (useful intelligence) ›

Fact A single, published "NBCUniversal Responsible AI Principles" policy document did not surface in public search. What is visible is governance in practice, internal roles, legal/privacy/cyber guardrails, accelerator themes, rather than a posted set of public principles.

Inference, and a real opening for you The absence of a public, codified responsible-AI framework for the HR function is itself the opportunity this role is pointing at. If they had it locked, they would not need someone to own "governance and performance metrics for HR transformation." On the call, this is where you can be genuinely curious: ask what governance they already have for AI in the people function, and where they feel it is thin. That is a peer question, and it is exactly the seam you would own.

The HCM stack reality

NBCU O&T is on a greenfield SuccessFactors build ›

Fact NBCU's Corporate Operations & Technology group is running an HR transformation that includes a greenfield SAP SuccessFactors Employee Central implementation (hire-to-retire). The surrounding application portfolio named in their own job posts includes SuccessFactors, SmartRecruiters, and ClickBoarding, with SuccessFactors Onboarding and Employee Central at the center. They are staffing architects, engineers, and business analysts around it right now.

This is the single most useful operational fact in this brief. It tells you what "HR transformation" concretely means inside this org: a live, global HCM re-platforming.

Sources: Built In (Sr Business Analyst, SuccessFactors), SmartRecruiters (Director, Software Engineering, SAP SuccessFactors).
How to handle the Workday-vs-SuccessFactors gap honestly ›

Inference / framing Do not pretend SuccessFactors experience you do not have. The move is to name the transferable spine: you have led a global enterprise through exactly this class of change, moving off a legacy HCM onto a new cloud platform, including an Oracle-to-Workday migration and a new Workday rollout. The platform label is different. The transformation, data migration, change management, governance, and adoption are the same problem you have already solved once.

Framing line you can use "I have not run SuccessFactors specifically. I have led the same transformation on Workday, including a migration off Oracle. What carries over is not the button-clicks, it is knowing how these re-platformings actually stall, and how to keep adoption from dying after go-live." That is confident, honest, and directly on their pain.

Caution: This brief adds context; it does not overwrite your prep page's fit-map. If your positioning page marks Workday as a strength, this is the same story told with one more fact attached: their tool is SuccessFactors, so lead with the transferable transformation, not the tool name.

Business context you can reference lightly

Peacock and the streaming economics ›

Fact Peacock reached about 46 million subscribers at the end of Q1 2026 (up from 44 million the prior quarter and 41 million a year earlier), on roughly $2 billion in quarterly revenue (up from about $1.2 billion a year earlier). It still posted a Q1 2026 loss of about $432 million, and NBCU media leadership signaled it was on track to turn profitable in Q2 2026.

Inference As a standalone company, Peacock stops being a line item propped up by a cable parent and becomes a core asset NBCU has to fund and grow on its own balance sheet. Translation for HR: headcount, capability, and workforce planning decisions get sharper and more scrutinized, because the streaming bet now sits on NBCU's own books.

Sources: The Hollywood Reporter, Dealroom.
How to use this on the call (do not over-index) ›

Inference You do not need to quote subscriber numbers at a recruiter. This context is for one purpose: to understand that NBCU is a company where every function is about to be judged on its own economics for the first time. Workforce planning under real budget pressure, which you have done, is more relevant than any streaming stat. Reference the moment, not the metrics.
