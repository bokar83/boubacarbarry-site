Why We Made Each Major Decision

Focus channel over Energy channel: Cultural moat. Anyone can make deep house. Nobody can replicate Guinea + Francophone + neural beats with earned authority. When you have a moat, you launch the moat first.

No avatar to start: Every competitor with 6M–34M subs runs without one. The research is conclusive. Sonic identity must be established before an avatar reinforces it. Avatar before sonic identity = designing a storefront before knowing what you sell.

Gumroad at $29: Skill listings in this niche range from $5 (too cheap, signals low value) to $99 (too high for an impulse digital buy). $29 hits the impulse purchase sweet spot. It is a 10-minute decision for a working developer. Bundle pricing keeps it compelling vs. individual listings.

ABORT skill over other skill ideas: It fills a real gap in the existing decision stack (Council + Karpathy), is a 1-file build, and has a natural Gumroad bundle angle. S effort, disproportionate compounding value.

Gen X angle deferred to content, not product: The ICP angle needs validation through content performance before building a product around it. Start with 3 posts, see if Gen X audience responds. Then develop the product.
