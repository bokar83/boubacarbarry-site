Campaign structure (how to set it up)
  • One campaign, ABO (budget set at the ad-set level, not campaign level). No Advantage+ campaign budget.
  • Two ad sets: "DIRECT" and "FREE." Each holds the same 3 ads (Card A/B/C + their primary text).
  • Objective: DIRECT = Landing Page Views. FREE = Leads. (Set the objective to match at the campaign/ad-set level per Meta's current flow.)
  • Targeting (both ad sets, identical): Salt Lake County + 15 miles around Sandy, UT. Location only. No age filter, no interests, no demographic layering. The image does the targeting.
  • Placements: Advantage+ (automatic). No manual placement picks.
  • Special ad category: none (a paid workshop is not housing/employment/credit/politics).
  • Destination: DIRECT ads → catalystworks.consulting/w/ads. FREE ads → catalystworks.consulting/w/free. That link is the only difference between the two ad sets.

Fastest build: create the DIRECT ad set with all 3 ads, publish, then Duplicate the ad set, rename it FREE, and change only the destination link on its 3 ads. You never rebuild a creative by hand.

Win / kill rule (per track)

Do not judge early. No kill decision on either track before it has spent at least $50 OR run 3 full days, whichever is later. Inside that window the numbers are noise.

TrackPrimary judgeEarly read
DIRECTCost per seat (Stripe Purchase)Cost per Landing Page View, then link CTR
FREECost per lead, THEN lead → seat conversion rateCost per lead alone (before seats show up)
KEEP / scale the track producing seats at the lower true cost per seat. Free can win on cheap leads but still lose if those leads never buy, so wait for the lead → seat number before crowning it.
KILL / reallocate a track once past the $50-or-3-day floor if its cost per seat is clearly worse AND its early reads (CPLPV / cost-per-lead) are in the worse half. Move its budget to the winner.
Hard stop: if total spend hits $300-400 with zero seats on both tracks, pause and revisit offer/creative before spending more. There is no cost benchmark for this account yet, so read the first 2-3 days of real numbers before calling any figure "too high."

Free invites (comp seats you hand out 1:1)
Your comp-seat link: catalystworks.consulting/w/vip
SHARE 1:1 ONLY · DO NOT POST PUBLICLY

When you want to give someone a seat for free, send them this link personally (text, DM, email). It opens a short page ("Your seat is on me"), takes their name + email, confirms a July 30 seat with no charge and no card, and adds them to your list. It fires a CompleteRegistration pixel event, so comped guests stay separate from paid buyers and from free-track leads.

Why it is a separate link and not on the site. The page is set to noindex and is linked from nowhere public. That keeps a free door from cannibalizing the $47 signups. Only people you personally send it to will ever find it. After they RSVP, you email them the room and a calendar hold.
