▸A Opening: Inciting Incident & the Corrected Mixed Signal

The discovery call opens by listening for the inciting incident and the mixed signals. In v2, the owner's answers sharpen the mixed signal into something the documents alone could not show.

Q.What made you decide to look at this now? What is the inciting incident?
A.Roughly ten weeks of full build with zero consistent revenue, capped by a flagship paid workshop that sold zero seats against 350 ad clicks. But the deeper trigger is a decision: he job-searched February to May, got no traction, and committed fully to Catalyst Works. Now that it is the only boat, the question "why is it not working" became urgent and personal.
Q.Walk me through the current state in one breath.
A.A complete business on paper: brand, a four-rung offer ladder, six-plus live Stripe links, a Calendly with qualifiers, five landing pages, a 1,846-line audit tool, lead magnets, playbooks, and a researched strategy. Stage, per the company's own Notion hub: "$0 to first contract." No closed paying client in any source. And the owner is now all in, full-time, unlimited hours.
Q.Where do the signals not add up? What is the mixed signal?
A.This is where v2 sees what v1 could not. The owner converts in person ("when they work with me, things go really well") but is not in the rooms, and when he is in front of someone, about 70 percent leave not knowing what he does. So the mixed signal is: strong closer, zero closes. The capability is there, the commitment is there, and the revenue is still zero, because the first conversation leaks at clarity and then at the ask. The gap is two feet of conversation, not capability and not direction.

▸C The 8-Lens Diagnostic (re-run on the owner answers)

One diagnostic question per lens, re-answered with the owner's live input. The governing rule: the lens follows the constraint.

Q.Theory of Constraints. What single bottleneck, if elevated, releases the most flow?
A.The in-room clarity-and-conversion rail. He converts once working with someone and is now fully committed, so the one node that gates everything is the first conversation, which leaks at clarity then at the ask. Elevate that and flow begins.
Q.Jobs To Be Done. What job is the buyer hiring this for, and does the offer speak to it?
A."Find and fix the margin I am losing to broken operations." The documented positioning nails it. The problem is not the words on paper, it is that he never says them the same way twice in person. The locked offer fixes that.
Q.Lean. Where is the waste?
A.Re-improvising the message, in pixels (four website re-skins) and in person (a fresh ramble every conversation). Maximum motion, no locked output. Lock the message once and the waste stops.
Q.Cynefin. Is this complicated or complex?
A.Obvious-domain. The fix is known and mechanical: a locked offer, a memorized pitch, a 3-step ask, drilled. Not knowing what to do was never the problem.
Q.Behavioral Economics. What pattern is driving the behavior?
A.Working-memory overload from an improv-prone, connection-seeing mind. Given an open question with no rail, the connections fire in every direction and he loses the thread. In his words: "My ADHD is hurting them and me." This is a structure problem, not a discipline or courage problem.
Q.Systems Thinking. What rail is missing?
A.The conversational rail. He already built an external rail for his work (an AI operating system he runs his business on) precisely so he would not have to hold it all in his head. He never built the same rail for the sales conversation. Copy his own solution into the one place he never applied it.
Q.Design Thinking. Has he watched himself talk to the user?
A.Now, yes, honestly. He named the failure himself: ~70% leave unclear, ~30% are never asked. That self-observation is what made the real constraint visible. The product was being explained, never landed.
Q.AI Strategy. Is the unique AI advantage being used as proof?
A.Not yet, but it is the fix. He runs his own business on autonomous agents, the most credible demo in his market. Packaged as the in-room live demo (Step 2 of the rail), it is proof, consultative close, and ADHD anchor in one move.

▸E The 5-Whys Chain (owner-confirmed, to the real root)

v1 drove this chain to "an unmade commitment." The owner's answers reroute it: commitment is made, so the chain continues past it to the structural root, confirmed in his own words.

The chain, driven to the owner-confirmed root

Why is revenue zero?
Because almost no one he meets becomes a client.
Why does almost no one convert?
Because he gets in front of people but loses the room. ~70% leave not knowing what he does; the ~30% who get it are never asked.
Why do they leave not knowing what he does?
Because he has no locked, simple offer, so he improvises the explanation every time.
Why does improvising fail?
Because his mind sees connections across domains and fires in every direction. Unanchored, the improv spirals into tangents and he loses his own thread. "My ADHD is hurting them and me."
Why does he never reach the ask?
Because the ask lives at the end of a clear thread, and he never reaches clarity, so he defaults to "let's talk more."
Why has this never been fixed?
Because he has been trying to fix it with discipline (try to be clearer, remember to ask) instead of structure. Willpower cannot do a rail's job.
Root: an improv-prone, connection-seeing mind running the most important conversation in the business with no external rail. The ask-failure is downstream of the clarity-failure, and the clarity-failure is downstream of the missing rail.

The commitment chain (v1's root, now closed)

Was the in-person motion never run because attention was divided?
That was the v1 read. The owner corrects it: he is now all in, full-time, boats burned.
So does fixing commitment fix the revenue?
No, and that is the key finding. He is committed and still converts zero, because commitment was never the binding constraint, the rail was. Removing the commitment question exposed the real wound.
Resolution: v1's "unmade commitment" is closed by the owner's answer. The chain does not stop there; it continues to the missing rail, which is where it always actually ended.
The non-obvious bridge (his superpower, turned inward) The trait that makes him a great diagnostic consultant, cross-domain connection-spotting, is the same trait that loses him the room. Pointed at a client's problem (the problem is the rail), it is a gift. Pointed at an open "so what do you do" with no rail, it is a tangent machine. The fix is not to dull the gift, it is to give every important conversation a rail so the connection-engine runs on a track. He already proved he can do this: he built an AI operating system to externalize the structure his work needs. The rail is that same move, applied to his mouth.
▸F The Council Reruns & Premortems (against the new fact)

Five independent voices, re-run in premortem mode against the fact that commitment is made. Five rounds, written as challenge and response. The job: re-rank the constraint and premortem the new fix.

Round 1 -- the new fact enters: commitment is made

The Contrarian
Challenge: the precondition is gone. So what?
Removing the excuse exposes the wound. He is all in and still converts zero, because the moment he is in front of a buyer he loses them. Commitment was never going to fix a mouth with no rail. v1 was hiding the real problem behind a decision that was already being made.
First Principles
Challenge: which v1 assumption just broke?
"The in-person motion converts if you run it." It does not. He has been in front of people and 70 percent leave not knowing what he does. There is a hidden node between "in the room" and "closed," and it is clarity.
The Outsider
Challenge: what does an outsider see now?
A prospect who cannot say what he does cannot buy and cannot refer. The second part is the killer: zero clarity equals zero word of mouth, so distribution can never compound. Every confused conversation is a dead end, not a seed.
The Expansionist
Challenge: what is the instinct to resist?
"Get in more rooms now that he is committed." Wrong order. Pour rooms into a broken rail and you burn rooms. Build the rail first, then the same rooms start producing. The rail is the multiplier; rooms are the volume.
The Executor
Challenge: name the always-visible pattern.
Four website re-skins is the same ramble in pixels. He cannot hold one message in his mouth or on the page. The fix was always "lock one sentence and make it un-improvisable." True in March, true now.

Round 2 -- re-rank the constraint

Convergence
With commitment demoted to resolved, two candidates remain: distribution (not in the rooms) and conversion (loses the rooms). Conversion is binding first, because rooms are relatively cheap to enter but every room is wasted, and the referral layer poisoned, if the conversation has no rail. Distribution is binding second. The convenient answers stay dead: not the ads, not the price, not "no strategy," and now also not "he is not committed."

Round 3 -- the owner-confirmed root

The 5-Whys, confirmed
No locked offer leads to improvising, improvising spirals because of how his mind connects, the spiral loses the thread, the lost thread never reaches the ask. The voices agree on the distinction that matters: this is a structure problem, not a discipline problem. "Be clearer and remember to ask" has failed for months because it asks willpower to do a rail's job.

Round 4 -- premortem the new fix (the rail)

Premortem A
"We built the rail and he still rambled." Why?
He never drilled it, or abandoned it mid-conversation when a connection sparked. Mitigation: drill out loud daily to automatic; keep it to three steps; make the live demo (Step 2) the physical anchor he returns to when the spiral starts.
Premortem B
"The rail killed the warmth that closes." Why?
Over-scripting suppressed the consultative style that wins. Mitigation: the rail is a skeleton, not a script-read. Lock the opening, the middle move, and the close. Free to be consultative between the rungs. Rails on the turns, freedom on the straights.
Premortem C
"Rail fixed, but he never got in enough rooms." Why?
ROI-gated travel got read as "stay home." Mitigation: local rooms are free, podcasts are remote and free; only conferences cost money and those are ROI-gated. No budget excuse to be roomless.
Premortem D
The v1 precondition, re-tested: "He took a job after all." Why?
This no longer fires. He job-searched Feb to May, got no traction, burned the boats. The v1 binding precondition is closed and recorded as closed, not carried as a live risk.
Chairman synthesis -- convergence ~93% With commitment resolved, the binding constraint is the in-room clarity-and-conversion rail: the missing external structure (one locked offer, a memorized pitch, a three-step consultative ask) that lets his connection-seeing mind sell instead of wander. Distribution is the necessary second move, gated behind the rail. The fix is a built artifact, drilled to automatic, with the live demo as its anchor and his consultative style preserved between the rungs. Every convenient answer, including v1's own "unmade commitment," is retired with evidence.
