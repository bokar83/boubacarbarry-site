"Tools change. Principles don't."
The locked message
Promise

Walk out understanding how AI actually works, so you can pick up any tool, today's or next year's, and put it to work without starting over.

Core

AI tools change fast. I teach the principles that transfer to any LLM (Claude, Gemini, open models, whatever is next), not one product. I went from ChatGPT to Gemini to Claude, and other tools since, and never lost a step, because I kept the principles, not the product. The burn = betting your business on a tool that will change. The protection = owning the principles.

Why it converts

Teaching the thinking (not the tool) kills the "looked like the free Claude class" risk. Taught so simply they love it, come back, and teach it to others. Teach-back solidifies learning + fills the next room.

Principle stack — the transferable backbone
  1. An LLM predicts from what you give it. How you direct it (context + clear instruction) is the real skill.
  2. Give it your context once, reuse it (your "brain file" / memory).
  3. Turn what you repeat into a saved recipe you own (a "skill").
  4. Agentic = it DOES multi-step work, not just answers. You delegate, not just ask.
  5. Pressure-test + simplify your thinking (Decision Council + Simplicity Check = meta-skills that ride any tool).
  6. Own it, move it. Never get locked in.

The floor (if everything else breaks)
Minimum viable session

The Decision Council, built live + premortem bonus. One owned artifact + one unforgettable proof beats a feature parade. Design to wow a SMALL room (8 people) — do not depend on 30 bodies for energy.

Dossier Drop (confirmed)

Pre-research every registrant → one-page brief per attendee ("what AI could do for YOUR business").

Delivery = both, staged: hand each attendee THEIR OWN brief at their seat (private, personal, take-home wow). Demo the tool building one live on a volunteer or sample business — never a real attendee's gaps on the big screen (privacy).
