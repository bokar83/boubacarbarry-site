How this page works, and what it cannot do
  • Edits, approvals, rejections and notes are all local only. This is a static page with no backend. Nothing you click or type is saved, not on this device and not anywhere else. Refreshing or leaving the page loses all of it.
  • Approve and Reject are per item. Click one to mark it, click the other to change your mind. Only one can be active at a time and the item's border changes color so the state is visible at a glance.
  • The notes box under each item is for you. Type what should change, or why you rejected it. It is plain text, nothing formats it.
  • Copy My Decisions, top of the page, is the only export. It builds a plain-text summary of every item's current state and its notes, copies that to your clipboard, and that is what you paste into chat. Nothing on this page reaches anyone until you do that.
  • Copy puts the block on your clipboard exactly as it reads in the box at that moment, including your edits.
  • Reset to original puts a block back to the approved wording if an edit goes sideways.
  • Nothing here has been sent or posted. This page is the only place this copy lives outside chat.
