HR Revenue Plan — Sequenced Go-To-Market

2026-06-24 · Built from 4 viability verdicts + market research · Plan only, no code
One audience, one ladder, three rungs of spend. A $29 ER-investigation pack is the top of the funnel for everything — it climbs to a $15K mid-market tool and a recurring consultant membership, each rung sourced from the rung below. The moat through all of it: deep HR practitioner depth in ER investigations — the one thing no generic creator or SaaS company can fake.

The Ladder (individual → corporate)

  • Pack buyer ($29 digital product) — an individual HR pro or fractional consultant feeling ER-documentation fear. Buys to de-risk their own AI use.
  • Tool buyer ($8K–$15K/yr app) — the same person or their org, who hits enough ER cases that templates aren't enough and they need a structured system.
  • Recurring / corporate — fractional consultants (membership) + mid-market orgs (tool seats), with dashboards bundled into the tool.

Every buyer is segmented at purchase (in-house / consultant / EU-exposed). That segmentation routes them to Phase 2 (tool) or Phase 3 (membership). The pack feeds the whole machine.

Phase 1 — ER Investigation Pack

GOShip firstFastest cash

Lowest build complexity, highest edge-fit, make-once/sell-many. Ship in ~7 days, first sale by day 10. A working ER-investigation system, not a cheat sheet.

What is literally in the pack

  • 30 compliance-framed prompts by investigation stage, each with a one-line compliance note + a "what could go wrong" flag:
    • Intake — complaint intake script, allegation framing, conflict-of-interest / investigator-suitability check, scope definition, evidence-preservation notice
    • Planning — investigation plan generator, witness-list builder, interview-order logic, evidence map
    • Interview — question banks for complainant / respondent / witness (three distinct banks), open-then-closed sequencing, credibility-assessment framing, note structure
    • Documentation — contemporaneous-notes formatter, finding-of-fact (fact vs. opinion), preponderance-standard application, disparate-impact / protected-class language check
    • Findings & closure — findings-and-report drafting, outcome determination, remediation/recommendation
    • Communications — complainant + respondent outcome letters, manager comms, no-retaliation reminder, case-closure summary
  • Defensible templates (editable Word/Doc): intake form, investigation plan, witness interview (with attestation block), timeline builder, findings/report template (the court/EEOC-survivable centerpiece), outcome letters, PIP template
  • Compliance checklists: pre-investigation readiness, mid-investigation completeness, documentation-defensibility ("would this survive discovery?")
  • 1-page "use these safely" guide — the human-oversight layer; this page itself is a compliance artifact
  • Credentialed-author framing on the product page — signals practitioner, not creator

Pricing

$19 founding (first 50) → $29 standard. Easy unapproved-budget yes for a pro already paying SHRM $299/yr. No refund/guarantee language.

The hook: EU AI Act liability, not productivity. Lead with accountability + the Aug 2, 2026 high-risk-employment deadline (max penalty €15M). A pack with a documented human-oversight protocol becomes a defensible paper trail — "compliance evidence," not a "time-saver." Doubles the defensible price and opens an EU-exposed second segment. Core line: the HR practitioner remains accountable for AI outputs.

Launch

  • 1 LinkedIn post, one angle: the liability angle (~400 words). Open with the fear, name the Aug 2 2026 deadline, include one free sample prompt (the findings-documentation one), close with the link. First person, Boubacar voice, no hype.
  • 1-page Carrd/Gumroad page, title stuffed with niche keywords: "The HR ER Investigation AI Prompt Pack: 30 Compliance-Framed Prompts for Documenting Employee Relations Cases." One free preview prompt.
  • Seed where HR pros gather (zero-cost, credentialed): Resources for Humans Slack (~23K HR leaders) — 10 free review copies to named SHRM-CP/SPHR holders for on-record quotes; People Geeks (~15K); SHRM local chapters + Boubacar's HR LinkedIn network. Credentialed testimonials are worth ~10x a generic review.

Consulting feeder

Every pack includes a one-page "When you hit an edge case" insert + Calendly link to a paid 30-min session at $150–$250. One $29 sale converting to one $500–$2,000 engagement turns LTV from $29 into $529+. Use the existing boubacarbarry Calendly event.

Demand gate. PASS = 10 paying buyers at $19 in 14 days → build the suite. AMBER (3–9) = reframe, retest once. FAIL (<3) = scratch suite (pack still feeds consulting). Bonus: 2+ EU-exposed buyers validates the compliance frame and unlocks the $297 team bundle.

Suite sequence (only after pass): ER Investigation → Job Descriptions → Performance Reviews → Comp Letters → Policy Writing. Each standalone + suite member; bundle 5 for $97.

Phase 2 — ER Investigation System (app)

GOHeavier build

Demand STRONG: 45% of ER practitioners still on spreadsheets, 27% track nothing, misconduct claims at a 9-year high (14.7/1,000). Pricing desert between $0 and ~$40K/yr. Do not build until Phase 1 passes — Phase 1 buyers are the warm demand list.

Positioning: mid-market, below HR Acuity

  • Target: HR teams at 200–2,000 employees with an ER function but no purpose-built tool.
  • Price wall: HR Acuity ~$40K/yr (73.6% enterprise), NAVEX $40K–$120K/yr, AllVoices (mid-market but navigation + surprise-fee + anonymity complaints).
  • Deliberate $8K–$15K/yr ($2–$5/employee/mo band). ROI: avg EEOC settlement ~$40K = one avoided case pays 3–5 years.
  • Improve play: take AllVoices' slot, fix its complaints — structured workflow, no surprise fees, private by default.

Lean MVP (intake → timeline → evidence → report)

  • Standardized case intake form
  • Timestamped timeline log
  • Local evidence attachment (file-path reference, no upload)
  • Report generator (reuses the Phase 1 report template — direct asset carryover)
  • Case dashboard (open/closed, investigator, days open)

Cut from v1: anonymous hotline, HRIS integration, AI interview questions, multi-tenant, cross-case analytics. Shape: local Electron / self-hosted, no cloud for the core loop. "It worked": 5 paying teams complete one investigation end-to-end, confirmed defensible by their legal.

Moat: local/private + EU

  • Local-first / air-gapped — ER data is the most sensitive in the org; a real edge for regulated industries and legal.
  • Legal e-discovery structure in the report (chain-of-custody, exhibit numbering, redaction flag) — reframes as a litigation-defense asset. Top breakout lever.
  • EU AI Act compliant-by-design (local + human-in-loop audit trail + FRIA checklist) — opens EU mid-market from Q3 2026; incumbents won't retrofit cheaply.

Channels: attorney referral + in-house legal budget

  • Employment-attorney referral (low CAC): ~10 attorneys advising 200–2,000-employee firms; offer a read-only counsel dashboard + referral deal. They can't recommend $40K HR Acuity to a 300-person client — this fills a gap they feel in discovery. Their endorsement makes "defensible" credible.
  • In-house legal as budget holder (payer ≠ user): pitch the risk / litigation-defense budget line, not HR tools — larger and stickier.
Demand gate. Smoke test before build (the "45% on spreadsheets" stat + lander): PASS = 30 qualified signups (HR Mgr+, 200–2,000 employees) in 30 days. Build-greenlight: 3 paying customers + 1 investigation completed end-to-end before investing in EU + attorney channels.

Phase 3 — HR-OS pivot + Dashboards reframe

PIVOTGated

Both returned PIVOT. Neither builds until its Path-to-GO gate is met. These are the recurring-revenue and platform layers on top of the proven Phase 1/2 base.

HR-OS → fractional HR consultant membership

Why it pivoted: as an in-house individual membership it hits thick free competition (SHRM, free AIHR/HiBob templates). The pivot moves the buyer to the fractional HR consultant (5–10 SMB clients) — proven WTP (ConnectsUs runs this at $58/mo for years), clearer ROI, real gap (SHRM templates aren't white-label).

  • v1: AI-safe HR usage policy template (US+EU, white-label) + the Phase 1 ER prompt pack re-framed "consultant version" (asset reuse) + monthly "AI employment law" email via Kit + small founding community.
  • Delivery: Kit email, not a SaaS platform. The monthly drop IS the product — zero platform friction, existing stack.
  • Pricing: $49/mo individual, $149/mo consultant (white-label, 5 clients), $299/mo firm. Founding $29/mo for first 20.
  • Breakout (after 20+ members): PEO distribution — one deal at $499–$999/mo ≈ 10–20 subs (via NAPEO + LinkedIn).
Path-to-GO: 10 paying founding members at $29/mo within 14 days of one LinkedIn post + Carrd waitlist targeting fractional consultants specifically; members confirmed as consultants (not in-house). Build nothing before the 10 payments.

Dashboards → reframe / bundle

Why it pivoted: "local-first privacy" is no real edge — Power BI Desktop is free, local, same exports. Standalone subscription churn is high (build dashboard → done → stop paying).

  • Route 1 — reframe to "zero-setup, 30-minute HR dashboard from your export." Real pain is config complexity (74% data-quality issues, 69% lack analytics skills). Moat = Stripe-style zero-config column auto-detection.
  • Route 2 — healthcare HR vertical, where local-first IS a real differentiator (HIPAA BAA exemption) and budgets are larger.
  • Route 3 (recommended) — bundle into the Phase 2 tool. ER case data feeds dashboards; bundle = the only local mid-market HR analytics platform ($11K–$21K/yr). Raises ACV, lowers CAC, kills the "Power BI is free" objection by selling it as an add-on to a tool they already value.
Path-to-GO: Bundle route (preferred) — during Phase 2 smoke test, offer a dashboard demo; >50% say "I'd want both" → build bundle. Standalone fallback — 40+ signups where >50% are NOT already on Power BI, OR 10+ fractional CHROs confirm $200+/mo. Column-mapping auto-detection is the make-or-break feature.

How it ties together

  • Phase 1 pack is the single top-of-funnel. Every buyer segmented at checkout: in-house / consultant / EU-exposed.
  • Pack buyers qualify the tool (Phase 2): anyone who buys AND opens repeat ER cases is a warm lead. Same report template the tool generates — they already trust the format. Phase 2's smoke test runs against this list.
  • Pack buyers qualify the membership (Phase 3): the "consultant" checkout segment is the direct founding-member target. No cold audience.
  • Dashboards attach to tool buyers: sold as a Phase 2 add-on, inheriting qualified buyers.

A $29 individual purchase climbs to a $15K corporate tool and a recurring membership, every rung sourced from the one below. The ER expertise is what makes the $29 pack credible enough to start the climb.
