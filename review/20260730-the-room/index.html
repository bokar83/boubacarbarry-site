05Handling rules, one screen

Eight people carry a flag. Most of them are simply "do not guess". Two are real and the detail is deliberately not on this page.

  • Two off limits topics. Robert Maxwell has a personal matter flagged. Francisca Alohan has a religious affiliation flagged. Neither is written down here on purpose. Stay on the professional framing each of them chose.
  • Greet with no name at all. scoutent, tt8_ut, sdajhana. The identification is not solid enough and a wrong name is a bad open.
  • Do not guess Kim's surname. She was added today at Bill's request. Greet her as Kim.
  • Do not assert a relationship. Julie and John McDonald share a surname and two minutes. Ask, do not tell.
  • Do not assume a role. The MailBeacon registrant may not be the founder.
  • Do not reference Lana Monson's job search, or any specific customer review of Aspen Roofing, good or bad.
  • Nothing came from people search or background check sites. No home addresses, phone numbers, ages, relatives or financial data are on this page or in the research behind it. Where such a site was the only source, the fact was dropped and the entry left unresolved.
