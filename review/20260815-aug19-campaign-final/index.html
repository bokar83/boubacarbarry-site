Angle: the most common objection ("I tried it, it gave me garbage, I stopped") reframed as a missing-context problem, not a broken tool.

LinkedIn
The most common thing I hear is some version of this: I tried it, it gave me garbage, I stopped.

I believe you. And the garbage was accurate, given what it had to work with. You asked a question that only makes sense if the thing already knew what you sell, who buys it, what you charge, and what you have already ruled out. It knew none of that. So it wrote you a paragraph that could have been about anybody, and you correctly concluded it was useless.

Fixing that is not a prompt trick and it is not a better tool. It is giving it the context you would have given a new hire on day one, once, in a file you keep and reuse.

That is the whole hour on Wednesday. You bring the business, we build the file, you leave using it.
