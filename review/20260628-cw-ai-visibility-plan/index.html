01 Why Businesses Are Invisible to AI Search

Understanding the mechanism is more valuable than following steps. If you understand why, you can adapt when platforms change.

The most common misconception: "I need to ‘get into’ ChatGPT or Perplexity." Wrong model. AI search engines are synthesizers — they don’t have independent knowledge of your business. They search the web, read top results, and generate a response citing what they found. Not in those results? You don’t exist to them.

Why This Matters

All AI visibility optimization is really search optimization with an extra layer. You cannot “hack” your way into an AI engine’s response — you have to earn the underlying search ranking that AI engines draw from.

The Two Visibility Pathways

Real-Time AI Search · 30-90 Days
Track A + B
Gemini, Perplexity, ChatGPT Browse actively query the web when they respond. Fix your search rankings → they find you.
Training Data · 6-18 Months
Track C
ChatGPT (no Browse), Claude offline respond from frozen training data. Cannot be changed fast. Start now; measure quarterly.

The Engine Mechanism Map

Google Search ───────────────→ Gemini AI Overviews + Search Grounding Bing Search ───────────────→ Perplexity (primary index) ───────────────→ ChatGPT Browse (Bing API) ───────────────→ DuckDuckGo (Bing backend) Training pipelines ──────────────→ ChatGPT (no Browse) · Claude offline · Llama
The Big Insight

Fix Bing → unlock four AI surfaces simultaneously (Perplexity, ChatGPT Browse, DuckDuckGo, Bing). Fix Google → unlock Gemini. No separate optimization needed per AI engine — fix the underlying sources they all draw from.

Why Three Tracks

Most “AI SEO” services focus only on Track B — treating the symptom, not the cause. Track A is the foundation that makes Track B possible. Track C is the insurance policy. All three are required for durable visibility that survives algorithm updates.

Topical Authority — The 90-Day Concentration Rule

One cluster. All 90 days. No diversification until month 4. For Catalyst Works: “AI implementation for Utah SMBs.”

Why Concentration Wins

A site with 20 articles on one topic outranks a site with one article each on 20 topics. The topical authority effect compounds — each new piece lifts every other piece in the cluster. Slow for 4 weeks, then accelerates. Resist covering trending AI topics or unrelated services until Month 4.

03 Day 1 Research — Before Any Content

Keyword Validation

  • Export all 30 audit prompts as keyword strings
  • Run through Google Keyword Planner AND Semrush (two tools cross-check each other)
  • Filter: keep only keywords with 10+ monthly searches
  • Record: monthly volume, keyword difficulty (KD 0-100), CPC (commercial intent proxy)
  • Rank by commercial intent × volume → pillar page targets
  • Secondary terms (50-500 searches, KD <30) → cluster post targets
Why KD Under 30 + CPC Signal

A new site cannot rank for KD 70+ keywords in 90 days regardless of content quality. KD <30 means ranking within 90 days is realistic. High CPC ($5+) signals that buyers — not just browsers — use those queries. Publishing to 0-search keywords wastes all execution effort.

PAA Research (AlsoAsked.com)

People Also Ask boxes appear on 75-85% of informational queries. These are exact AI prompts. AI Overviews frequently pull from the same pages that answer PAA questions.

  • Run AlsoAsked.com on all 5 pillar keyword targets
  • Export full question tree (50-100 questions per seed)
  • Cluster by intent: awareness / consideration / decision / post-purchase
  • Select top 20 by volume + fit → FAQ entries with 2-tier answers
  • Map each question to its host content piece before writing begins

Entity Stack
Day 1 to 7
Why Entity Signals Before Content

When 10 different websites consistently describe “Catalyst Works” as an AI consulting firm in Salt Lake City, Google’s knowledge graph treats it as a verified entity and cites it confidently. Inconsistent or absent entity signals reduce AI citation regardless of content quality. Entity work is faster to implement and index than content.

Person + Organization JSON-LD

  • LinkedIn title: “AI Implementation Consultant | Catalyst Works | Utah SMBs” — include value prop, not just job title
  • Same title across: LinkedIn, Twitter/X, GBP, Calendly, Crunchbase, HARO bio
  • Person JSON-LD on About + Organization + LocalBusiness JSON-LD on homepage
  • sameAs array in all schemas linking to Wikidata, LinkedIn, Crunchbase, Clutch

Wikidata Entry — The Knowledge Panel Unlock

Why Wikidata Is the Highest-Leverage Action Most Businesses Skip

Wikidata is a free database that Google’s Knowledge Graph explicitly treats as authoritative. A Wikidata entry triggers the Knowledge Panel (business card in Google’s right column) within 2-6 weeks. Knowledge Panels signal to AI engines that the entity is authoritative enough to cite by name. Takes 30 minutes. Most competitors have not done this.

  1. wikidata.org → “Create new item” → label “Catalyst Works”, description “AI implementation consulting firm in Utah”
  2. P31 (instance of) → Q4830453 (business enterprise)
  3. P159 (headquarters) → Q23337 (Salt Lake City)
  4. P856 (official website) → https://catalystworks.consulting
  5. Note the Q-number assigned → add to sameAs array in all JSON-LD schemas

NAP Consistency (Name, Address, Phone)

Inconsistency across directories fragments your entity. Write canonical NAP — exact name, address format, phone format — never deviate. Target: 40-60 consistent citations by Day 90 via BrightLocal + Neustar Localeze + Data Axle.

φ1
Technical Foundation
Day 1 to 14
Why Technical Before Content

A perfectly-written blog post targeting an ideal keyword still won’t appear in AI search if the page takes 8 seconds to load or has no title tag. Technical issues are multipliers — they suppress every other investment you make.

Title Tag Formula

[Primary Keyword] for [Persona] in [City, State] | Brand

Example: “AI Implementation Consulting for Utah SMBs | Catalyst Works” — under 60 characters, primary keyword in first 30, unique on every page.

Why the Formula Matters

AI engines inherit their understanding of page content from how Google categorizes it. Google categorizes pages partly from title tags. “Home” tells Google nothing. The formula tells Google exactly what the page is, who it’s for, and where it operates — determining whether Gemini cites it for local AI queries.

Meta Description Formula

[Pain point]. [Local trust signal]. [CTA]. — under 155 characters.

Example: “Most Utah SMBs leave 30% revenue on the table from manual processes. We show you exactly where AI pays off — book a free 20-min scan.”

Core Web Vitals — Fix Sequence (Mobile-First)

  1. Preload hero image — <link rel="preload" as="image" href="/hero.jpg" fetchpriority="high">. Fixes LCP. Drops load time 0.5-1.5 seconds.
  2. Self-host fonts — eliminates DNS lookup delay from Google Fonts external request.
  3. Defer non-critical JavaScript — defer or async on analytics, chat widgets. Lets the page render first.
  4. Cloudflare CDN (free tier) — serves files from geographically close edge nodes. Utah visitor gets files from SLC node, not a server in Virginia.

Target: LCP <2.5s, CLS <0.1, INP <200ms on mobile.

Why Pillar-Cluster vs. Standalone Blog Posts

Google cannot identify expertise from scattered unrelated content. The pillar-cluster model creates a topical web: each cluster post links to the pillar, passing authority. The pillar accumulates authority from multiple cluster posts and ranks for broad keywords. Broad ranking signals topical expertise, lifting all cluster posts. Each piece makes every other piece stronger. Slow for 4 weeks, then accelerates.

Pillar 1: AI Implementation for Utah SMBs — 2,000+ words. Pillar 2: AI Visibility for Local Service Businesses — 1,500+ words.

φ3
The 2-Tier Answer Architecture
Applied to ALL Content — The Core AEO System
Why a Special Architecture for Answers

Traditional content is narrative — paragraphs that flow into each other. AI engines extract short, self-contained passages to answer specific questions. If passages aren’t clearly bounded, the engine skips your page and extracts from a competitor’s. The 2-tier architecture makes content both human-readable AND AI-extractable simultaneously.

Tier 1 — 40-60 Words — PAA Target + Voice + Featured Snippet

A complete, self-contained answer in paragraph format (NOT a list). First phrase restates the question. Stands alone — remove everything else and Tier 1 still answers. Voice assistants read this verbatim. PAA boxes display this. AI Overviews excerpt this.

Tier 2 — 150-250 Words — AI Overview Passage Target

Expands Tier 1 with mechanism (why this is true), evidence (example or data), and nuance (the exception). Bold 2-3 key phrases. Include 1 outbound citation to a credible source — AI engines are more likely to cite pages that cite credible sources themselves.

Definition Pages (AI Citation Magnets)

Why Definition Pages Are a Separate Content Type

AI engines are heavily used for definitional queries. A definition page doesn’t pitch — it teaches. Teaching pages get cited by AI engines far more often than sales pages because they’re more likely to be the most accurate, comprehensive answer to the definition query.

  • “What is AI Implementation Consulting?” — primary awareness entry point
  • “What is a Fractional AI Operations Consultant?” — differentiation query
  • “What Does an AI Audit Actually Show You?” — pre-sale objection handler

Scenario-Based FAQ

Traditional FAQs answer questions. Scenario FAQs match situations — how people actually describe problems to AI engines. On homepage AND primary service pillar: “Which situation fits you right now?” — 6 scenarios, each: “If you’re experiencing [symptom], here’s what you need” in 2-tier format.

φ4
Schema Implementation
Day 7 to 21
Why Schema Matters for AI Citation

Schema (schema.org) tells search engines what content is about — explicitly, not through inference. When Google sees FAQ schema, it knows those are questions and answers and can extract them for AI Overviews. A page with explicit FAQ schema is more likely to be extracted than an identical page without it.

  • FAQPage + Question + Answer — FAQ master page, bottom of every blog post, homepage scenario section. JSON-LD in <head>.
  • HowTo + HowToStep — “How to Diagnose Whether Your Business Has an AI Implementation Gap” (5 steps). Appears in rich results for process queries.
  • Speakable — homepage + About page only. CSS selector targeting .hero-summary. ≤60 words, one sentence, answering “What is Catalyst Works?”
  • LocalBusiness + Service — homepage. Connects business to service type + geographic area for local AI queries.
  • BreadcrumbList — all cluster posts. Signals content hierarchy to Google’s algorithm.

Always validate at Google’s Rich Results Test before publishing. Broken schema = zero benefit.

05 Pre-Mortem: Why This Plan Fails

Name the failure modes before starting so you can prevent them.

Failure 1 — Most Likely

Content execution stalls at week 3. Cluster posts require 2/week for 6 weeks. If this slips, authority doesn’t accumulate.

Prevention: batch-write 4 posts in Week 1, schedule in advance, never write week-of.
Failure 2

GBP review asks don’t happen systematically. 15 reviews requires asking every single week.

Prevention: weekly Thursday calendar event, template saved, send within 24h of every interaction.
Failure 3

Schema is added but broken. JSON-LD errors are invisible to the author but visible to Google — broken schema = zero benefit.

Prevention: test every schema at Rich Results Test before publishing. Non-negotiable.
Failure 4

Keyword validation skipped. Publishing content targeting 0-search keywords wastes all execution effort.

Prevention: keyword validation is Day 1, blocks all content writing until complete.
Failure 5

Link building never starts. Without referring domains, pillar pages don’t rank; the stack collapses.

Prevention: 2 HARO pitches/week as recurring calendar event, same priority as client deliverables.
Failure 6

Topical drift — writing trending AI topics outside the cluster at week 5.

Prevention: content calendar review every 2 weeks. Kill or defer anything outside the cluster until Month 4.
Failure 7

Measurement not reviewed weekly. First 45 days of feedback invisible; course-correction at Day 60 too late.

Prevention: Monday weekly review, 15 minutes, non-negotiable. The weekly scan fires automatically — the review must also fire.
