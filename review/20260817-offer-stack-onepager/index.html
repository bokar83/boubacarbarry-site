TL;DR

The Voice Engine. A founder gets five things in a fixed order over 21 days, and each one is what makes the next possible: a recorded working session, a written voice guide built from it, three finished pieces published in his own voice, a prompt pack his team can run, and a one-page weekly process for keeping it going.

What he owns at the end: his voice written down, three pieces live, and a repeatable way to keep publishing without hiring anyone.

The price is not on this page and it is not decided. Only Boubacar names it, and he names it live on the call. Any number sitting in an older document is a recommendation, never a decision.

Section 4 has the whole thing as plain text, ready to paste into Slack.

1. The stack, five pillars in the order they happen

Say the bold line for a fast prospect. Add the line beneath it when the prospect wants the step by step.

1. One recorded working session, 60 minutes.

I ask, you talk. That hour is the only time I need from you.

2. A written voice guide, built from that session.

How you actually think and talk, on paper. Your positions, your phrasing, the things you refuse to say.

3. Three finished pieces, published in your voice.

The guide is what makes them sound like you and not like a ghostwriter. Two rounds of revisions on each.

4. A prompt pack your own team can run.

The same instructions I used, handed to you, so the next piece does not wait on me.

5. A one-page weekly process.

The rhythm that keeps it going after I step out. Something you run in a half hour a week.

What you own at the end: your voice written down, three pieces live, and a repeatable way to keep publishing without hiring anyone. That is the part that stays.

Same five pillars, different depth. One sentence each for a bull or a tiger. The full step by step for an owl or a sheep.

2. Term, what starts it, and what is not included

How it starts. I write and deliver the first piece before any money moves. You read it, then you decide.

Term. 21 days from the working session. One payment. Nothing renews, so there is nothing to cancel.

Not included. I do not run your accounts and I do not write replies as you. No design, no video, no ads, and no distribution beyond where you already post. Revisions cap at two rounds per piece.

Capacity. Three founders at a time, and that is a real number, not a tactic.

5. One question for Devin: the pieces, or the whole engine

Devin's own written outreach system says that once you run the engine for yourself you can run it for a client, and he calls that a bigger, stickier offer than I'll write you some posts. Running the engine means prospecting, the profile, the content, the outreach, and the daily rhythm. That is a materially larger offer than five deliverables in 21 days.

The trade-off, stated plainly. The five-pillar version is finishable, priceable in one sentence, and it ends on a delivery date instead of asking a stranger for a retainer. The whole-engine version is worth more per client and is far harder to leave, and it asks a founder to hand over a live channel to somebody with no case to point at yet.

The recommendation. Take the five-pillar version into the call as the offer, because it is the one that can close, and ask Devin directly whether the engine should be the offer instead. His answer is worth more than a unilateral restructure, and the stack does not need to change to ask the question.
