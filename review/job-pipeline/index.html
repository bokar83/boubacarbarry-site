Guardrails for anything written off this page
  • Never claim you ran HR or operations, and never call yourself an executive. You worked in HR, helped run things within HR, and were operations-adjacent only.
  • Never “15 years at a Fortune 100.” The shape is ten years in global HR leadership for a Fortune 100 company, GE Healthcare then Gas Power out of Atlanta then four years in Dubai, with Dubai the last four of that same ten. Never ten plus four.
  • People leadership is real but interim. You stepped in as the people leader for months at a time in Atlanta and repeatedly in Dubai. Say it that way. Neither “no direct reports” nor “managed a team” is accurate.
  • Helped lead the Kaizen. Never led, never ran, never a bare co-led.
  • Preserve the hedges. Close to two thousand employees. About four to five hundred. Almost every country, not every country.
  • Lead with the how. On site, walks the floor, PPE in the car, safety first. Your resume hides this and it is what separates you on a shortlist.
  • Public city is Salt Lake City. Never South Jordan.
  • The AI work in MENA was personal and shared informally. Not a programme. The formal credential is the global HR skills initiative delivered as part of the enterprise HR transformation.
  • No coding claim. The system exists, it runs the business, you designed and operate it. That is the true and stronger version.
