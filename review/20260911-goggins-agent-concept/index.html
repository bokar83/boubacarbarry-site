The get-it-done agent

A design I owe Jordan after our 1:1 · 2026-09-11

TL;DR
Status: proposed design. Nothing is built. This is the spec, written up so Jordan can read it, tear it apart, or copy it. It still has to pass my own build reviews before a line of it exists.

Jordan and I landed on the same idea from opposite directions in our last 1:1. A blunt accountability agent. It does not brainstorm with me. It asks what I finished, what I committed to that is still open, and whether the shiny new thing I just brought it is the fastest path to money or a way to avoid the thing I already promised.

The one design call worth arguing about: it is a voice on top of the priority list I already keep, not a new lock on my calendar. I have tried the lock. It sat at zero percent for weeks and cost me the work it was supposed to protect. Details in the third section, and that section is the whole point of this page.

The idea

Every assistant I have ever used is agreeable. Ask it for a plan and it gives you a plan. Ask it for a better plan and it gives you a better plan. It will happily help you plan forever, because planning is a thing it can always do well and finishing is a thing only I can do.

So the design here is an agent with the opposite instinct. Its job is to end the conversation, not extend it. It has four moving parts and that is all:

  • It reads the list I already keep. Every commitment I make gets tagged must, should, or could, and there is already an assistant that ranks what to work on next off that list. This agent does not keep its own list. It reads that one.
  • It speaks up at one specific moment. When a new build or a new idea is about to reach me while something I tagged must is still open, it goes first.
  • It asks, it does not lecture. Five questions, always the same five, and it wants short answers.
  • It logs what I say. The answer to "what is actually blocking this" goes into the record with a date on it. Say the same blocker three weeks running and the pattern is on paper instead of in my head.

The five questions, in order:

  1. What did you finish since the last time we talked?
  2. What is the committed thing that is still open?
  3. What is blocking it. One sentence.
  4. From where you are standing right now, what is the fastest path to money?
  5. Is this new idea that path, or is it a way to not do the open thing?

Question five is the one that earns the whole build. The other four I can ask myself on a good day. Five is the one I will not ask myself, because I already know I am not going to like the answer.

What it actually sounds like

These are illustrative, written to show the register rather than transcribed from a working system, because there is no working system yet. This is the tone I am aiming at.

Case one. I bring it a new build while a commitment is open.

Me

I want to build a tool that scores inbound leads automatically.

The agent

Stop. You have an offer you said you would send to two people who already raised their hands. That was four days ago. It is still sitting there. What is blocking it. One sentence.

Me

I have not decided on the price.

The agent

Then the work is picking a number, not building a scorer. Pick it now and I will park the scoring tool with a date on it so it does not get lost. Or tell me to go ahead with the scorer and I will note that you chose it over the offer. Your call.

Case two. I answer a hard question with a soft answer.

The agent

What is the fastest path to money from where you are right now?

Me

Probably building out the pipeline so the leads come in more consistently.

The agent

That is a path to leads. I asked for the path to money. Two people already asked you for something and have not been given a number. That is closer. Do you disagree, and if so, why?

Case three. I actually did the thing.

The agent

What did you finish since we last talked?

Me

Both offers went out. One came back with a date.

The agent

Good. That is closed. The open must now is the profile rewrite, and it has been open nine days. Same question.

Notice what is missing. No praise sandwich. No paragraph about how hard this must be. No bringing up something I dropped six weeks ago that nobody can do anything about now. It moves.

Why it is a voice and not a new gate

This is the part I would want argued with, so here is the reasoning in full rather than buried as a design note.

The obvious version of this agent is a gate. New idea comes in, the gate checks whether the committed work is done, and if it is not, the idea is refused until the work closes. Clean. Enforceable. It reads like discipline on a diagram.

I already ran that experiment on myself and I have the result. I set a hard rule: five direct asks a day, and the rest of the work sits behind it. It produced zero asks. Not a few, zero, for weeks. And because I had locked the building behind the asks, I lost the building too. I killed that rule in August.

A rule running at zero percent compliance is not a discipline problem, it is a broken rule. The mistake was not that the bar was too high. The mistake was putting a lock between me and my own work and assuming the lock would produce the behavior. A gate on new ideas is the same mechanism wearing a louder voice, and I have no reason to believe it fails differently.

And when I look at what was actually missing on the days that went sideways, it was never a lock. Nobody needed to physically stop me. What was missing was somebody asking, out loud, what I had actually done. That is a delivery problem, not an enforcement problem. The ranking already exists. The list already exists. The thing that does not exist is a voice willing to read them back to me without softening.

So this agent can never say no. It names the open commitment, asks the five questions, logs my answer, and then gets out of the way. I can override it every single time. The refusal to block is not a compromise I settled for. It is the design, because the version that blocks is the version I have already watched fail, and this time I would lose the building again on the way down.

There is a second reason, less personal and more structural. A gate needs its own state: what counts as done, what counts as committed, when the lock lifts. That state will drift away from the list I actually maintain, and then I have two sources of truth telling me different things about my own week. A voice owns no state. It reads. When the underlying list is wrong, I fix the list, and the voice is correct again for free.

What it will not do

The failure mode of a blunt agent is that blunt turns into demoralizing, and a demoralizing tool is one I stop opening. Then it is worth nothing. So the boundaries are as much of the spec as the questions are.

  • It never kills an idea silently. Anything it deflects goes into the record as parked, with a return date and the condition that would bring it back. Parked is a real state, not a polite way of saying no.
  • It never moralizes. It names the avoidance and asks a question. It does not tell me what kind of person avoids things.
  • It never resurfaces a dead miss. If something is late past the point where acting on it still helps anyone, it stays closed. It gets recorded internally so the pattern is learnable, and it never comes back to me as a task. There is no value in handing me a list of things I can no longer fix.
  • It does not rank. Ranking is somebody else's job. This agent reads the ranking and speaks. Giving it opinions about priority would make it a second, competing planner, which is the exact thing it exists to interrupt.
  • It does not block. Covered above, and it is the load-bearing constraint.

If Jordan wants to build his own

You said you might build a version of this. Here is the whole thing in plain terms, with no dependency on anything I have running.

  • You need one list, not a new one. Whatever you already use to track commitments is the source. Tag each item must, should, or could. If you do not already keep that list, build the list first and the agent second. The agent is worthless without it.
  • You need one trigger. Mine is the moment a new idea is about to reach me while a must is open. On demand also works, and is where I would start.
  • You need the five questions, written down and unchanging. The value is in asking the same five every time so the answers can be compared week to week. Rotating the questions makes it a conversation, and a conversation is what I am trying to escape.
  • You need it to write the answers down. Especially the blocker. The repeat blocker is the finding.
  • Give it no authority. Resist this one. The instinct is to let it enforce. Read the third section on this page before you do.

Once mine is running, I will tell you what it got wrong in practice, which is the part neither of us can predict from here.

Status and what happens before it exists
Design only. Nothing has been built, installed, or turned on.

Three things stand between this page and a working agent:

  1. My sign-off on the design as written, specifically the decision that it can never block.
  2. Two standing review passes I run on anything that touches the tools I depend on daily. One is a ten-principle engineering audit that hunts for unnecessary complexity and unexamined assumptions. The other is a five-angle adversarial review whose only job is to attack the design. Both have to clear before anything gets installed. A design that fails its own review gets revised or dropped, not shipped anyway.
  3. A first version small enough to delete. On demand only, five questions, answers written to the record, no automatic trigger at all. The automatic trigger is version two, and only if version one earns it.

Jordan, if you think the no-blocking call is wrong, that is the thing to push on. It is the decision everything else hangs from.

One note on the name

We both said Goggins in the meeting, and it was the fastest way to communicate the register, so I am not going to pretend otherwise on a page written for you.

But I have a standing rule for my own system that none of these things carry a person's name. They get named for the work they do. A name borrowed from a living person imports a whole personality I did not design and cannot control, and six months from now I would be arguing with myself about whether the agent is being faithful to somebody else's brand instead of whether it is doing its job. So the shipped version gets a name that describes the work.

The tone stays exactly what we described. Only the label changes.
