Why one week apart, and why the Wednesday/Thursday shape mattered originally

When these two sessions were first designed in August, the online one was deliberately put on a Wednesday and the in-person one on a Thursday. The reasoning is on the record and it was not aesthetic: a competing Thursday event seven days before the Thursday in-person session invites a straight head-to-head comparison between a $25 seat you attend from your desk and a $50 seat you drive to at night. Wednesday made them read as a sequence instead of as alternatives.

The reschedule had briefly put both sessions on Thursdays before this was ruled on. Wednesday September 16 for the online session (decision 1, now settled) restores the original design: online Wednesday, in-person Thursday, eight days apart.

05Timeline, working backward

The rule this timeline is built on, and it is written down

After the August 19 session was cancelled at zero, the retro recorded the cause precisely: a 9-day, 15-asset campaign was BUILT on 2026-08-11 and days 1 through 4 were never posted — not blocked by a tool, just unowned. The first promotion of any kind went out four days before the event. The rule that came out of it: promotion starts no later than three weeks out, and a campaign is not done when the assets are written. It is done when every asset is a dated row with a named channel and an owner, sitting where a scheduler picks it up. An unposted campaign is indistinguishable from no campaign.

Three weeks before September 16 is Thursday August 27. That is why week one below is a decisions-and-unblocking week rather than a posting week, and why it cannot slip.

08What "done" means, and how it gets checked
  • A campaign is done when every asset is a dated, queued row with an owner — not when the copy is written. That is the one lesson the August cancellation paid for.
  • Silence and success look identical here, which is why the two drift checks on September 2 and 9 are on the plan rather than left to attention. They report counts, never reassurance.
  • A missed day is not backdated. Stale dated content going out late reads worse than silence. That call is already made and recorded.
  • Every link that ships gets opened in a real browser at desktop and at 375px, with the final URL after every redirect confirmed and the UTMs checked through the chain. A 200 on the wrong page is still a 200.
  • The number that counts is paid seats, not reach, not clicks, not registrations. Both sessions sit at zero today and every prior push has ended at zero.
