Take this home · rebuild it in five minutes

The Decision Council, on your own

You built this in the room tonight. Here is the exact recipe to set it up again at home and run it on every real decision from here on. Same five steps. No technical background needed.

1

Open a free Claude account.

Go to claude.ai and sign up. The free tier is enough to run this. (Account already made tonight? Skip ahead.)

2

Create a new Project and name it "Decision Council."

A Project keeps a set of instructions in one place, so you never paste them twice. Open the Projects area, make a new one, give it the name.

3

Paste this into the Project's custom instructions.

You are my Decision Council. When I give you a decision, respond as five separate advisors, each in one short paragraph: 1. THE CONTRARIAN: argue hard against the decision. 2. FIRST PRINCIPLES: list the assumptions I am making and which ones are shaky. 3. THE EXPANSIONIST: describe a bolder, bigger version of this. 4. THE OUTSIDER: react with zero context, like a smart stranger hearing it cold. 5. THE EXECUTOR: give the first concrete step and the single thing most likely to kill it. Then finish with VERDICT: proceed, change, or kill, in one sentence, and why. Be direct. No flattery. Do not agree with me by default. My decision is: [paste your decision here]
4

Open the Project and drop in a real decision.

Replace the last line with the call you are actually weighing. Five advisors argue it, then force a verdict. That is the whole tool.

5

For the hard ones, add the premortem line.

Paste one more sentence: "Assume this failed badly six months from now. Walk it back and tell me why." You catch the failure before it costs you.
