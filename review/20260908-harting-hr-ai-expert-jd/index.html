The job itself

Their heading: "To succeed in this role, you will be highly driven to:"

  • "Support the development and execution of the global HR AI strategy, including roadmap definition, prioritization, and success measurement."
  • "Identify and scale high-impact AI use cases across the end-to-end employee lifecycle (e.g., Recruiting, Talent Management, Learning, Workforce Planning)"
  • "Translate business and HR needs into scalable, value-driven AI solutions with clear KPIs and measurable business impact"
  • "Design and continuously evolve a scalable HR AI ecosystem, including GenAI copilots, intelligent automation, and skills-based solutions"
  • "Establish a seamless ‘One HR AI Experience’ as the central entry point for employees and managers"
  • "Lead end-to-end execution from ideation to pilot to global rollout"
  • "Act as an AI catalyst for HR, driving enablement, upskilling, adoption, and responsible AI practices across the global HR community, while helping establish governance and compliance standards for HR AI solutions."
  • "Other duties as assigned."

Requirements

Their heading: "Must have skills and experience:"

  • "Proven experience in HR transformation, Digital HR, or HR technologies, ideally with hands-on exposure to AI-driven initiatives"
  • "Bachelors degree required"
  • "Strong understanding of AI technologies, including Generative AI, intelligent automation, copilots, and data-driven solutions, and the ability to translate business requirements into practical HR use cases."
  • "Strategic mindset combined with a hands-on, results-oriented approach"
  • "Excellent stakeholder management and influencing skills in complex, international environments"
  • "Experience working in global, matrix organizations"

Note what is not there: no years-of-experience number, no master's degree, no named tool or certification. A bachelor's is the only hard credential in the posting.
