Your turn · minute 22 · the part you came for

Build your Decision Council

Build live · keep forever

Paste a real decision you face tonight. Five advisors pressure-test it in thirty seconds, then force a verdict.

1. THE CONTRARIAN: argue hard against it. 2. FIRST PRINCIPLES: which of my assumptions are shaky. 3. THE EXPANSIONIST: the bolder, bigger version. 4. THE OUTSIDER: a smart stranger hearing it cold. 5. THE EXECUTOR: first step + the one thing most likely to kill it. Then VERDICT: proceed, change, or kill. Be direct. No flattery.
Bonus: "Assume this failed badly in six months. Walk it back."
