2. Industries with acute pain right now

Real 2026 signal, not guesses — see Sources for every citation.

"67% of U.S. HR leaders say reviewing AI-generated applications has slowed their hiring process — one in five report delays of more than two weeks. 65% say skills have become harder to verify in AI-optimized résumés." — Robert Half survey, March 2026
"Recruiters who once sifted through 80 applications are now looking at 400 … a 2025 Gartner survey projects that one in four candidate profiles worldwide will be fake by 2028." — multiple 2026 trade sources (see Sources)
  • HEALTHCARE STAFFING — a mid-size health-system-adjacent staffing operation can see 500+ applications/month against 40+ open reqs; a 3-person recruiting team works out to ~167 applications/recruiter. Over 50% of healthcare staffing agencies now lean on AI tools for recruiting, but most cannot produce the audit trail EEOC's 2026 algorithm rules require.
  • LOGISTICS / WAREHOUSING / 3PL — fast-growing 3PLs hire in bursts (peak-season surges, new facility openings) and post volume swings sharply month to month, exactly the pattern that makes an AI-application flood hardest to triage.
  • RETAIL CORPORATE (HQ, not stores) — seasonal + corporate hiring surges stack on top of each other; corporate teams are lean relative to headcount need.
  • FINANCIAL SERVICES BACK-OFFICE — a January 2026 lawsuit against Eightfold (an AI hiring platform used by companies like Microsoft and PayPal) alleges undisclosed AI "likelihood of success" scoring; banks face real compliance exposure (NYC Local Law 144: up to $1,500/violation/day; Maryland AI Employment Law: up to $10,000/violation) with no internal AI governance team to manage it.
  • PROFESSIONAL SERVICES (accounting/consulting scaling firms) — talent-shortage pressure (CPA pipeline declining) collides with AI-flooded applicant pools, and most mid-size firms have zero internal AI evaluation capability.
  • TECH / SaaS SCALEUPS (100–800 employees) — fast headcount growth in eng/sales roles draws the highest volume of AI-polished applications, and a lean People team has no bandwidth to build a screening process from scratch.
