5-Round Council Verdict
"The ads" is a convenient single cause. The real root cause is a cold-traffic funnel with nothing warm behind it.
350 people saw the page. Zero converted. That is not primarily an ad copy problem. It is a trust-gap and sequence-of-operations problem. Personal asks never went out. The borrowed-list channel was never executed. The page had zero social proof. Ads cannot compensate for all three at once. They amplify a working funnel. They do not create one from scratch.

01 What We Did Well

Honest inventory. What was genuinely shipped or solved.

Operations fully de-risked

Venue secured at SLCC Miller Campus at lower cost than the initial scope. Insurance source found. Full run-of-show documented. Gear list confirmed. Entry logistics handled. Water-bottle placement figured out. None of this needs re-solving for July. That is real capital carried forward.

Content plan was strong

The 3-skill session structure was fully designed with a live-build component. Attendees would have left with something they built. The differentiator is real: showing a live autonomous operator handling actual business tasks, not a demo of what AI could theoretically do. AP does not have this. Nobody in the Utah market has this. It stays sharp for July.

Back-end offer stack built ahead of schedule

Three conversion paths existed after the workshop: free AI audit with a working Calendly link, $497 cohort, $297 kit. Most workshop operators build the back-end after their first event. Having it ready on day one is ahead of where most operators are. July can open on these immediately when seats fill.

Meta ads proved demand exists

350 people clicked through from cold ads to the seat page. The topic has real demand in the Utah market and the ad copy generated traffic. The ads failed to convert, but they diagnosed exactly where the funnel broke: after the click, not before it. That is useful signal, not wasted money.

Showed up and recorded

Most first-time workshop operators cancel when seat count is zero two days before the event. Running the session and recording it was correct. The recording is now the most valuable asset in the funnel. Every fix below starts with it.

02 What We Did Not Do Well

No softening. Each gap was concrete with a concrete consequence.

Distribution Gap 1

LinkedIn Connections.csv was never provided. The highest-trust personal invite channel generated zero invitations. The people exist. The channel simply never activated.

Distribution Gap 2

No direct personal asks went out. IC Slack members, warm contacts, colleagues -- none received a human message saying "I'm running this Thursday, would you come?" Most first events fill through this channel. It was skipped entirely in favor of paid ads.

Timeline Gap

Date locked 6/05. Marketing plan done 6/12. Event 6/25. That is 13 days. Chambers and SBDC need 2-3 weeks minimum. Meta ads need 7+ days to optimize. The slow channels were structurally unable to deliver on 13 days regardless of execution quality.

Ad System Gap

3 creative angles but no A/B test system. No conversion pixel -- Meta optimized for Traffic not Purchases. 350 bounces lost permanently to retargeting. No learning loop. Expensive guessing rather than scientific testing.

Social Proof Gap

First-ever event. The seat page had zero testimonials, zero past-attendee faces, zero "X people registered" counter. Cold audiences evaluating a $47 purchase from an unknown organizer need trust signals. None were present.

Borrowed-List Gap

AP's first advice: "borrow the list from local orgs." SBDC and chambers were contacted as passive co-marketers, not as list partners. The borrowed-audience playbook was never executed. This was the highest-leverage channel that stayed almost completely untapped.

Creative Quality Gap

Ad problems were identified early. No time and no forcing mechanism to fix them before the campaign ran. AP's feedback on the specific copy was not obtained before launch. The known problems ran anyway.

StartFEST Timing

StartFEST ran 6/23-24. Event was 6/25. A $47 close to strangers for a workshop the next evening is an extremely hard ask. The right use of StartFEST was list-building and seeding for July, not closing for a next-day event.

03 Why We Did Not Get People to Sign Up

This section goes deepest. Five rounds of Council analysis. The "no signups" vs "signups that don't show" distinction is addressed below.

Diagnostic clarity first There were zero signups -- not "signups who did not show." The breakdown was entirely at the conversion step, not the commitment or logistics step. There was no attendance problem. There was a purchase problem.

Root Cause Hierarchy (5-Round Council Output)

1
Personal asks never happened

IC Slack members, LinkedIn connections, warm contacts -- none received a direct human message asking them to attend. Most workshop operators fill their first 5-10 seats through exactly this channel. Personal asks are free, same-day, and convert at the highest rate of any channel. Paying $200 in ads while sending zero personal invites is reversing the order of operations entirely.

Heaviest
2
Cold-traffic funnel with zero social proof

The entire acquisition system was cold: cold ads pointing at cold audiences landing on a page with zero testimonials, zero past-event photos, no counter, from an unknown first-time organizer. AP's Meta ads work because AP has a track record and past attendees. For a first-ever workshop, cold traffic requires either exceptional social proof or a borrowed audience where an org they already trust has vouched for you. Neither condition was met.

Heavy
3
Wrong channel mix for the runway available

13-day runway is survivable with fast channels: personal asks (same day), warm network (1-2 days), borrowed list from a partner who can email immediately (3-5 days). It is not survivable with slow channels: chambers need approval cycles, SBDC has internal workflows, Meta needs 7+ days to optimize. The plan was built for a 3-week runway and executed on 13 days. The mismatch was structural, not a quality-of-execution problem.

Significant
4
Ad system underbuilt: no pixel, no A/B test, no retargeting

Meta optimized for Traffic with no conversion pixel. It found clickers, not buyers. The 350 who visited but did not convert were permanently lost -- no retargeting without a pixel. 3 creative angles with no test system means there was no way to learn which combination converts. Running 3 versions without testing is expensive guessing. AP's 3x3 = 9 variant system exists because you cannot know what works until you test it.

Real
5
Landing page not built for first-timer trust

A cold audience evaluating a $47 purchase from an unknown organizer needs: recognized social proof, past event evidence, a specific outcome statement, urgency signals, and a seat counter. The page likely had none of these. Even perfect ads cannot compensate for a page that fails to close the trust gap for cold traffic.

Contributing
Council Pressure Test -- Against the "Ads Are the Gate" Hypothesis

His hypothesis: "The ads are the gate. Better ads, properly A/B tested, would have filled the room."

Council verdict: Partially correct. Structurally incomplete.

Better ads plus A/B testing plus a longer runway equals meaningful improvement. True. But "ads fix everything" externalizes the problem to an improvable tool while leaving the harder problems untouched. AP's ads work because AP has social proof and a community. Better Boubacar ads pointed at the same zero-social-proof page still underperform. The personal-asks channel was skipped entirely -- not an ad problem. The borrowed-list channel was never executed -- also not an ad problem.

Corrected hypothesis for July: Ads fill the room IF the page has social proof from the recording, the borrowed-list channel runs first, personal asks go out simultaneously, and the ad system has a pixel, retargeting, and 9 tested variants. Ads are the amplifier. The other three are the foundation.

04 How to Fix It

Concrete and sequenced. Next week first, then July. The order matters as much as the individual steps.

Fix order matters Ads are step 4, not step 1. Sequence: (1) Build social proof from the recording. (2) Send personal asks. (3) Activate a borrowed list. (4) Run ads to amplify a funnel that already has momentum. Reversing this order again produces the same result.

This Week -- June 26-30

Social Proof Build By June 28
  • Clip 3 highlights from the recording: the live-agent demo moment, the framework intro, one insight that landed well. Post to LinkedIn and X. These clips are now the primary proof asset. They exist because the session ran.
  • Write and post the "I ran this with 0 attendees" story. Transparency about the result, what was learned, and what changes for July is a stronger authority signal than a polished recap nobody believes. This post builds trust with July's cold audience before they see an ad.
  • Collect testimonials this week from anyone who has engaged with the content positively. Even 3 LinkedIn recommendations changes the page conversion rate. Ask directly.

Goal by July 1: at least one visible proof artifact a cold audience can see and trust before clicking the July seat link.

Personal Asks By June 30
  • Send 20 direct personal messages to people most likely to attend or refer. IC Slack contacts, Utah-local LinkedIn connections, warm professional relationships. Direct message, not a broadcast. Human voice: "I'm running this again in July -- would you come, or do you know someone who should?"
  • Export LinkedIn connections now from LinkedIn settings directly. Do not wait for the CSV to be provided. Takes 10 minutes. Own this step.
  • Ask AP this week: "Would you share this with your Utah orbit in July?" He said he was open. Use the opening.
Meta Pixel Install By June 28
  • Install the Meta pixel on catalystworks.consulting now, before any July ads run. One-time setup. Starts building a retargeting audience from all future visitors including the 350 from June still within the retargeting window.
  • Set up a Purchase conversion event tied to the Stripe checkout confirmation. July ads optimize for buyers, not clickers.
AP Feedback Loop By July 1
  • DM AP: "Can I show you my ad copy and landing page? I want to see what you actually run." He offered. Use it this week, not the week before July's event.
  • Get at least one ad he runs. Even one Hormozi-style static with his copy structure is a better starting point than iterating from zero alone.
