Fintech Founder + Faucet Target List

Cold-outreach targets for the Fintech Founder's Authority play. 11 named founders + 5 faucets, each with a specific starting-point "leak" to open on.

Built 2026-07-15 Niche: fintech founders For: review → send to Devin Target list only — no outreach copy

How to use this sheet

Each "leak" is a starting point, not a finished line. It's a concrete, publicly-observable gap (strong company PR but the founder isn't leading a public POV; a category-defining personal thesis carried by the company account, not the founder; a great founder story sitting unused). Per Devin's method, do a 60-second live LinkedIn/X check on each person right before the outreach-workshop call to sharpen the exact leak — the first message gets built live on the confirmed leak. That's the method working, not a gap in the sheet.
Why these names, and who was left off ▶
  • The sweet spot: seed to Series A founders, heads-down or first-time, with a real story and a thin or inconsistent personal brand. Big, famous, well-resourced founders were skipped — they won't want authority help and already have a voice.
  • Deliberately excluded founders already running strong founder-brands (e.g. Itai Ben-Zaken / Honeycomb and Michael Spelfogel / Cardless both post actively). They're proof of the filter working, not targets.
  • The diaspora / immigrant cluster (Comun, Kredete, Afriex, Hizo) is intentional — that's the warmest bridge. The lived immigrant-operator story is the credibility unlock in those first messages.
  • The reframe to lead with: an operator who ran communications inside fintech; the lead magnet is the demo; founder-friendly case-study terms for the first few ("building my roster").
