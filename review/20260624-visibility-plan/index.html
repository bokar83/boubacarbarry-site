"The operator who installs intelligence into existing systems."

Not a consultant who talks about AI. Not a technologist. An operator who installs it: into business systems, into HR systems, into content systems, and into his own operating stack, documented in public. One identity. Multiple applications. The host's audience decides which application leads.

1The Spine

Throughline (every appearance, every room)

An operator who installs it: into business systems (the SMB consulting line), HR systems (the future-of-work line), content systems (the ghostwriting line), and his own operating stack (documented publicly). One identity, multiple applications.

Bridging line (adapt per show, never script-read)

"I build AI into operations: for businesses, for HR teams, and for myself."

One breath, three jobs: it names the practice (installs), names the three verticals (business, HR, myself), and positions the personal-brand angle (I use what I teach). It is the connective tissue between every appearance.

What this is NOT

  • Not a consultant selling strategy decks
  • Not an academic talking about AI trends
  • Not a tech founder pitching software
  • An operator who shows up, reads the system, and installs

2bGhostwriting as a Productized Service (the adjacency)

The ghostwriting line plays two roles in this plan, not one:

  • Inbound authority (current). Boubacar's own appearances build credibility that feeds the ghostwriting service. No dedicated shows needed while outreach is frozen.
  • Productized offer (the adjacency). The exact podcast machine he builds for himself (booking, guest prep, talking points, bio, follow-up, and the repurposing engine in section 6) is itself a sellable service: get the client booked on podcasts, ghostwrite their angle, bio, talking points, and follow-up, and turn each appearance into a content library. It extends the core promise from "your voice, written" to "your voice, on air and everywhere after." Same engine, sold as a service.
The proof angle: his own guesting doubles as live proof, a portfolio, and content fuel for the ghostwriting work. He sells the service by having run the play on himself first, the same "I built it for myself first" credibility that powers the agentic-OS hook.

Sequencing: this offer stands up AFTER the freeze lifts and the wedge proves the machine on Boubacar's own name first. Any offer page or sales copy passes voice + quality + lead-gen battery before it ships.
