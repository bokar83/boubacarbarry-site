The Roads Plan

Distribution and traffic go-to-market across every URL asset · Aug 19, 2026

TL;DR

The infrastructure is real and the roads to it do not exist. Six lead-magnet pages are live on humanatwork.ai with working email capture. Five of the six are linked from zero other pages on their own website. None of the six are in the sitemap. Two carry an explicit noindex tag. There is no way for a stranger to reach them. That is not a capture problem, it is a distribution problem, and it is the whole finding.

The recommendation in one line: build one Front Door, then point every road at it. A single page, humanatwork.ai/start/, that holds all six magnets behind one email field, reusing the capture pattern already proven live on the investigation pack. One URL to put in every bio, every footer, every post, every profile. Six competing URLs is why none of them get linked.

Eight roads, ranked by leverage per hour. Four of them are one-time builds that then run forever with nobody touching them: a shared footer link across the five brand sites, the sitemap and indexability fix, the UTM convention, and the Front Door itself. Three need his voice and should never be automated: LinkedIn comments, post approval, the newsletter. One is deliberately excluded: the faceless channels are anonymous by design and pointing them at his list would break that firewall permanently.

Cost to him in week one: about 35 minutes. Everything else is agent work on rails that already exist. This is deliberately not a new content operation. The north star is fractional engagements, not a media business.

One thing to know before reading further: analytics have been locked for non-payment since Aug 17, so every site is currently blind. The plan works around that with UTM-tagged capture rows, which cost nothing and work today, but the blindness is real and it is named in its own section below.

What this costs

Six finished products, each with a working persistence path into the real store, each solving one narrow problem, sitting behind a door with no street attached to it. The work is done. The distribution was never built. That is the exact shape of the complaint, and it is correct.

The other assets, honestly assessed

  • Five brand domains that do not link to each other. humanatwork.ai, catalystworks.consulting, geolisted.co, hrexposed.net, boubacarbarry.com. The mechanism: one Front Door, every road ends there

The teardown that started this thread is worth copying for its shape, not its niche or its volume. The shape is: one high-volume top-of-funnel channel, feeding one owned surface, feeding a cheap qualification purchase, feeding the real offer. Four stages, one path, no branching.

The current setup is the inverse. Six capture surfaces, five domains, no top-of-funnel road at all, and a tripwire nobody can find. The missing piece is not more capture and it is not another product. It is one destination.

The Front Door

Build one page at humanatwork.ai/start/. It presents all six existing magnets, takes an email once, persists it through the endpoint already proven on the investigation pack, and delivers whichever magnet the visitor picked. Every road in this plan terminates there. It is the one URL that goes in his bio, in every site footer, at the end of every post, and in his profile fields.

Why one and not six. Six URLs cannot be promoted. One can. The six stop being six competing offers and become the contents of a single offer. This also removes the which-one-do-I-link decision that is part of why none of them ever got linked.

Why humanatwork.ai and not the personal site. Three reasons, all concrete. The magnets already live there. The capture endpoint is already proven there against a real row read back out of the store. And the personal site's newsletter form currently posts to a contact webhook rather than a real subscribe, so building a front door on it would mean building on a broken form. Do not put the main entrance on the side of the building that does not work yet.

The eight roads, ranked by leverage per hour

Ranked deliberately. The top four cost his time once and then run without him forever. The next three cost his time every week and cannot be automated without lying about who is speaking. The last one is a road we are choosing not to build, and the reason matters.

Road 1. The shared cross-site footer. One-time build, permanent.

Five owned domains, none linking to each other. Add one shared footer block on every page of every brand site carrying a single line and the Front Door link. Ships through the GitHub-to-Hostinger auto-deploy each of those repos already has, so it goes live on push and then never needs touching again. Highest leverage per hour in the entire plan because the denominator is one hour, once.

Scope note: the four brand sites plus the personal site. Never the faceless channels or their email backbone, for the reason in Road 8.

Road 2. Make the six pages findable at all. One-time config.

Remove the noindex from the two magnet pages that carry it, add all six to humanatwork.ai/sitemap.xml, and link them from the site's own navigation as well as from the Front Door. Today this is the difference between invisible to every search engine and every AI answer engine, and merely reachable.

Honest limit: this makes them reachable, it does not make them rank. They are thin pages in a crowded topic and search is slow. The real value is that internal links plus a sitemap are what let the Front Door itself get indexed, and the Front Door is the page worth ranking.

Road 3. The publish rail, retargeted. Already built, needs a destination.

The content-board-to-auto-publish rail is the one genuinely automated distribution mechanism that exists and works today. The rail is fine. The posts it carries have no consistent call to action, so the traffic they earn goes nowhere.

The change is a standing rule, not a new system: every post on the board carries one closing line pointing at the Front Door, rotating which magnet it names. The board is already under a standing rule to be reviewed weekly and refilled every two weeks with two to three weeks of approved content, so this rides on a cadence that already exists rather than adding one.

Split clearly: the publishing is automatic. The writing and the approval are not, and must not be. Every post is shown to him inline and approved before it touches the board's queue state.

Road 4. The UTM convention. One-time, and it is what makes the rest falsifiable.

Every road gets a distinguishable tag so that a new row in the capture store can be traced back to the road that produced it. This works whether or not the analytics subscription comes back, because the capture endpoints already persist and already accept the tag. Without it, week two is a guess.

Road 5. LinkedIn comments. His voice, daily, already a standing obligation.

Three to five voice-gated comments a day is already a standing daily rule, so this road costs no new time. Two adjustments make it a road instead of just reputation work:

  • No links in the comments, ever. A link in a comment suppresses reach and reads as promotion, which is the opposite of the ally posture the rule is built on. The road is his profile: the website field and the featured section point at the Front Door, so every profile visit a good comment earns converts with zero extra work per comment.
  • Concentrate the target list on the AI-and-work voices, so the profile visits that result are the right audience rather than a general one.

This cannot be automated on the writing side and should not be. An automated comment on a well-known practitioner's post is a reputational liability, not a road.

Road 6. The newsletter, as a road and not only a destination.

Currently weak, and the only working broadcast path is a manual paste-and-send. Two moves. Each issue carries the Front Door link and names one specific magnet. And the issue archive is published as indexable pages, which makes the newsletter the only compounding organic asset in this plan: every issue is a page that can be found later by someone who was never on the list.

Road 7. Directories and communities. Small, one-time, worth two hours once.

Legitimate placements only: his own company pages, relevant newsletter directories, and the professional communities he is already a member of, where the rule is participate first and link only where the community explicitly permits it. Bounded to a single push, never an ongoing chore. Stated plainly so it is not oversold: this is the smallest road here. Two hours once, not two hours a week.

Road 8. The faceless channels. Deliberately NOT a road.

The faceless channel family is anonymous by construction. There is a separate neutral email backbone that exists specifically so those channels do not trace back to his identity. Pointing any of them at humanatwork.ai, or at any capture surface carrying his name, breaks that firewall permanently and cannot be undone once a viewer makes the connection.

They remain an adjacent revenue line. They are not top-of-funnel for his list. Any plan that counts them as traffic for the Front Door is wrong, and this one does not.

What genuinely runs without him, and what never will

This section exists because the ask was explicitly for things that run without him. Some of it does. Some of it cannot, and claiming otherwise would produce content that reads as a bot writing in his name, which is a rule violation and a reputational cost, not a shortcut.

Runs today, on rails that already exist, no human per instance

  • Auto-publishing an already-approved, already-scored post to LinkedIn and X on schedule.
  • The per-magnet welcome and nurture that fires when someone captures. Already wired.
  • Site deploys. Every brand repo auto-deploys on push, so a footer change ships itself.

One-time build, then runs forever with nobody touching it

  • The shared cross-site footer link.
  • The sitemap, noindex and internal-linking fixes.
  • The Front Door page itself.
  • The UTM convention.

Needs him, and should not be automated

  • LinkedIn comments. Judgment about what a specific post left out. Not automatable without producing exactly the generic filler the standing rule bans.
  • Post approval. A standing hard rule: every post is shown to him inline and approved before it is scheduled. An auto-published post he never read is a defect, not efficiency.
  • The newsletter send. The only working broadcast path is manual today.
  • Anything at all that speaks in his name. Every piece runs the voice battery and scores at or above the bar before it goes anywhere.

What is deliberately not in the cadence

Measurement, and the blind spot that has to be named

Analytics on every site have been locked for non-payment since Aug 17. Every query returns a subscription error, so all eight sites are currently blind. Separately, and confirmed earlier, the workshop funnel pages never carried a tracking token at all, so restoring the subscription alone would not have measured that funnel either.

That means today there is no way to tell which road works. A distribution plan you cannot measure is a plan you cannot correct, so this is not a footnote.

Two options, and the recommendation

  • Option A, restore the subscription. Gives page-level traffic back across all sites. Costs money and does not measure the funnel pages that were never instrumented.
  • Option B, count captures by UTM tag. Cruder, free, and works today, because every capture endpoint already persists a row and the tagging helper already exists. It measures the only number that actually matters here, which is people on the list, and it attributes them to the road that produced them.

Recommendation: do Option B regardless, this week, and decide Option A separately on its own merits. Option B is the one that makes the plan falsifiable, it costs nothing, and it does not depend on a billing decision. Option A is a nice-to-have on top of it, not a prerequisite.

The number to watch

New capture rows per week, split by road. Not impressions, not followers, not page views. If that number does not move in three weeks, the roads are wrong and the plan gets rebuilt rather than repeated louder.

What is not verified, and what is still open

Kept separate on purpose so nothing above has to hedge.

  • No audience numbers are quoted anywhere in this plan. No follower counts, no subscriber counts, no traffic figures. Not because they do not exist, but because analytics are locked and nothing could be verified today. A number that cannot be traced to a primary read does not belong in a plan that will be used to make decisions.
  • The newsletter's current list size and send cadence are unverified here. They affect how much Road 6 is worth and should be read off the platform before that road is sized.
  • Whether the personal site's newsletter form has been fixed is a separate open item that another lane may already be on. This plan routes around it rather than depending on it.
  • The Front Door's exact promise and name are open, per the decision above.
  • The analytics subscription is a money decision and therefore his, not an agent's. Named here rather than assumed either way.
  • Directory placements were not enumerated. Road 7 is scoped as a bounded two-hour push, and the specific list should be built at the time it is done rather than guessed now.

What this plan deliberately does not do

  • It does not build another capture page. Six work already.
  • It does not build another product. The tripwire exists and is proven.
  • It does not start a content operation. That is the wrong model for this objective.
  • It does not touch the faceless channels.
  • It does not automate anything that speaks in his name.
