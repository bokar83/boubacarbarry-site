4. CTA and funnel architecture

Primary CTA on every short: “Book a free 45-minute working session with Boubacar.” Link: calendly.com/boubacarbarry/free-ai-audit-owner-operators

The wording matches your positioning: assess what is stuck, identify bottlenecks and constraints, then determine whether AI or a system change creates the better payoff. It avoids selling AI as the answer before the diagnosis.

Secondary conversion path: place the full workshop behind an email form on the landing page. The form promise should be: “Get the full practical workshop: build a Business Brain, use a Decision Council, and run a Premortem.” After email capture, show the booking link immediately and include it in the delivery email.

Do not use two competing CTAs inside the short: asking viewers both to download the full video and book a call creates a choice point. The short asks for the call. The landing page uses the full video to capture and nurture people who are not ready to book yet.
