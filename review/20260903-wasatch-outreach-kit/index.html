Phone track · 101 companies

Cold Call Script

Read this in order. The gatekeeper line gets you routed, the pitch is what you say once you're actually talking to whoever runs operations, and the two objection responses cover the two most common ways this goes sideways.

If a receptionist answers

Hi, this is Boubacar Barry with Catalyst Works. Quick one, who handles operations or production planning there, the owner, a plant manager, whoever's closest to how a job moves from quote to the floor? If they don't know who that is: No worries, is there someone who'd know, or could you point me to whoever's been there the longest on the operations side?
Copied

The pitch, once you reach a decision-maker (30-45 seconds)

[Name], I'll keep this short. I work with manufacturing and distribution companies here on the Wasatch Front, usually the 10 to 50 person range, and most of them aren't losing margin because of a bad quote or a slow production line. They're losing it in the handoff between the two, the gap where an estimate turns into an actual job on the floor, where nobody's really watching it. I run a five day audit that goes in, maps exactly where that handoff breaks, and gives you a 30 day plan for the first fix, including where AI actually fits, if at all. If it doesn't find you a fix worth more than the fee, you don't pay. Is that worth a short conversation?
Copied

Objection: "Not interested"

Fair enough, a cold call is easy to be skeptical of. Can I ask, is it bad timing, or you've already got that handoff pretty dialed in? [If still no] Understood. Would it be alright if I sent you a one page rundown of what the audit finds, no follow up call unless you want one?
Copied

Objection: "Send me info"

Happy to. What's the best email for that? [capture email] I'll send over a one page breakdown of exactly what the audit finds and how it works, keep an eye out for it, the subject line will say "quote to floor handoff" so you know it's not spam.

Close

Can I put you down for a 20 minute call [day/time] to walk through what we'd actually look at? [If not ready] I'll follow up by email with that one pager, that work?

How to Work This List

Call first. The phone track is bigger (101 companies vs. 49 on email) and gives same-day feedback, so you'll know within a week whether the pitch is landing. Use the email track for the 49 UMA rows that carry a company address.

  1. Call the 101 DWS rows first, phone and address only. Work it top to bottom, or stay local for a day by filtering to one county on the seed list page.
  2. Email the 49 UMA rows that carry a company address, weekdays only.
  3. Log as you go. Add four columns to the CSV, or a copy of it: status, last_contact_date, next_action, notes. Status options: not called, no answer, gatekeeper, reached decision maker, not interested, info sent, call booked.
  4. Check it weekly. Count dials, connects, interested, and booked. If calls are landing under roughly one in five connects, or emails under a few percent reply, that's usually the list, not the pitch.
