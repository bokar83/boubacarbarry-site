The 5 questions
Angle 1 — core moat / origin story

1What's the one thing about how you got here that nobody in the AI-consulting space can copy, even if they wanted to?

Aim for the specific combination: 10 years in global HR leadership inside a Fortune 100, four of those years in Dubai, plus the fact you actually built a real AI operating system yourself instead of just talking about AI. Name a moment, not a resume line.

Why this angle: this is the thing every other cut (newsletter, LinkedIn, faceless) needs as its foundation — the "why you, and not the other guy" answer, told as a story with a day and a person in it, not a bio.

Angle 2 — current flagship test

2What are you testing right now, this month, that you don't yet know the answer to?

Point this at the live thing: the September workshops (Sept 16 online $25, Sept 24 in-person $50 in Sandy) and the $250 walkthrough audit sold inside them — the current pick in the 30-day cash plan. Say plainly what you're testing and what you'll do differently if it doesn't work.

Why this angle: it's the one piece of content that is actually dated and falsifiable right now, which makes it the most "building in public" cut — and it can carry a direct CTA to the Sept 16/24 seats without reading as a pitch.

Angle 3 — a real mistake, named plainly

3What's a specific decision you got wrong in the last 90 days, and what did it actually cost you?

Push for one dated, concrete example with a real before-and-after, not a generic "I learned to move faster" moral. A named mistake with a real cost is the difference between a story and a platitude.

Why this angle: this is the piece most likely to survive as a LinkedIn post on its own — candid and specific reads as credible in a way a polished win never does.

Angle 4 — the AI + HR thesis

4What is the one thing most HR and small-business leaders get backwards about using AI, and what should they do instead?

This is your expert-thesis answer — the thing you'd say if you had exactly one minute with a room full of owner-operators. Keep it to one belief, stated plainly, not a list of five tips.

Why this angle: this is the newsletter/thought-leadership cut and the one most likely to hold up as evergreen, since it's not tied to a date the way angle 2 is.

Angle 5 — the standalone hook

5If someone only ever heard one sentence from you, what would you want it to be, and why does it matter to you?

This is the deliberate hook-fishing question. Say it two or three different ways out loud until one version feels like it could open a post cold, with zero setup, and still land.

Why this angle: purpose-built for the "search test" — a short, quotable, standalone line is exactly what gets clipped, captioned, and tested as a hook across faceless-channel and LinkedIn cuts.

How to run the ~45 minutes
  • One camera, one continuous sit is fine — you don't need five separate takes, just five separate answers with a breath between them so cuts are clean.
  • Answer each question in your own words; if a question doesn't land right, restate it your own way and answer that instead.
  • Give yourself 2-3 minutes minimum per answer so there's enough footage to cut multiple pieces out of each one (a 15-second hook, a 90-second LinkedIn cut, a fuller newsletter excerpt).
  • After recording: agents cut/caption/publish across newsletter, LinkedIn, faceless-channel content, and the search-test piece — nothing publishes without your review first.
