Two things to know about the room itself

1. The 40-minute wall

us04 is Zoom's free cluster. It cuts calls at 40 minutes once 3 or more people are on. The meeting is 30 minutes, so it does not bite. But if Jim joins and the conversation is going well, the wall is real and it arrives without warning. If it looks like running long, say so at the 25-minute mark and book the follow-up rather than getting cut off mid-sentence.

2. The invite carries no passcode

Zoom personal meeting rooms usually require one, and if it is on, Bryce hits a prompt with nothing in the invite to type. Have your personal meeting room passcode in front of you and be ready to paste it into the chat or read it out. Better: open the room five minutes early and check.
