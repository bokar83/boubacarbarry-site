01 Your business brain
Kit Step 1: short answers

Type these into the kit's Business Brain step while your screen is up. Nine boxes, nine answers below, same order as the page. Kit Step 2 just copies the auto-generated prompt built from these, nothing new to type there.

1What your business is b_what

A 14-person commercial landscaping and snow removal company working the Salt Lake valley, two crews, owner-operated.

2What you sell b_sell

Weekly mowing and grounds upkeep contracts April through October, snow plowing and ice control contracts November through March, plus one-off seasonal cleanups and irrigation start-ups and blow-outs.

3Who buys it b_who

Small commercial properties: HOAs, strip retail centers, church parking lots, a few apartment complexes. Almost no single-family residential.

4The problem you solve for them b_problem

They do not want two separate vendors for summer grounds and winter snow, and they do not want a surprise bill the morning after a storm. One contract, one flat winter retainer, covers both.

5Your prices and packages b_price

Weekly mowing contract 220 to 340 a visit depending on lot size, billed monthly. Seasonal cleanup, spring or fall, 600 to 1,400 flat. Winter retainer 900 to 1,600 a month, November through March, covers plowing at 2 inches of snow and unlimited ice treatment. Plow-only with no retainer is 145 a push. About 1.8M a year total, roughly 60 percent summer grounds and 40 percent winter.

6How you talk to customers b_tone

Straight and plain, no sales pitch. Property managers want to know it is handled, not hear about it.

7Things you say a lot, and things you never say b_words

I say we will have it done before your tenants notice. I never say full service, state of the art, or eco-friendly.

8What you repeat every single week b_tasks

Writing the weekly route board for two crews, texting property managers when a snow push is running late, invoicing at the end of the month, chasing three or four accounts that pay past 30 days, checking the forecast every night November through March to decide if crews go out at 4am.

9Your biggest goal right now b_goal

Add a third crew for next season without taking on a partner or maxing out the equipment loan.

02 Your decision council
Kit Step 2: give it a real one

Step 1 of this station has no box to fill, just an instruction to open a new chat inside the project. The two answers below go into Step 2. The Bonus premortem line under Step 3 is fixed text in the kit itself, nothing to fill in there either.

10The decision actually sitting on you right now c_decision

Whether to take on a fourth apartment complex for the winter snow contract when the two crews are already tight on push time during a big storm.

11What it would not know on its own c_context

The complex is offering 1,400 a month, good money, but our last big storm took 6 hours to clear the routes we already run, and this one adds another 45 minutes per crew. If we say yes and blow a push time on an existing account, we risk the HOA contract that is up for renewal in March.
