Murray Area Chamber, my read

Worth approaching later, as a partner conversation, and not before the Aug 27 session has run. Right now you would be the challenger walking into a room somebody else already programs, with one in person session behind you. After Aug 27 you have two completed rooms and, if the standing asks land anywhere in this batch, a second chamber's endorsement to point at. That is a different conversation.

The angle that beats competing on price: their series is a recurring talk about AI. Yours is ninety minutes where members leave with three tools running on their own laptops. Those are complements, not substitutes. The honest pitch is the build session that follows somebody else's overview, which costs Murray nothing and costs their incumbent nothing either. That is a much easier yes than asking a chamber to switch vendors, and it does not put you in a fight you have no reason to pick.

One caution. Murray is the one organization in this whole set where a cold email can cost you something, because a clumsy approach gets repeated to the incumbent and to other chambers. It is the last one to contact, not the next.
