1. Verdict

Buy now, do not wait
GMKtec K8 Plus, ASIN B0DHNTW3H6 (32GB/1TB) — confirmed ~$739
~$39 over the stated "~$700" ceiling, but still the cheapest in-stock viable option among the three candidates checked (K8 Plus ~$739 vs Minisforum UM890 Pro $819.99–$975 vs DIY build $750–950). The machine is blocking income-producing work today, and there is no way to predict when — or whether — the next sub-$700 lightning deal fires. Historical drops recur roughly every 2–4 months but are not scheduled. $39 over an approximate ceiling is not worth losing weeks of productive-machine time waiting on a maybe.

If cash preservation outweighs urgency

Watch camelcamelcamel / Keepa directly (both blocked automated fetch this session — manual check recommended) for a dip back under $700; it has happened at least 3 times in the last 12 months, all dated 2025 (see §4, What Changed).
