Djeli needs a face on the home screen

It sits next to Instagram and Gmail today looking like an afterthought. Three directions below, each built from the same idea: Djeli is named for the West African griot, the keeper and speaker of a people's memory — which is exactly what a teleprompter does. Same family of warm, earth-toned marks; three different ways of carrying that idea.

TL;DR

Three icon directions for Djeli, each a hand-drawn warm-earth mark: A, The Kora Arc (instrument neck + resonator), B, The Griot's Voice (three rising arcs), C, The Djeli Mark (a doubled-line D). Each is shown at 512px, at 48px (the size that actually ships), as a maskable crop, and mocked up on a real dark and light home screen next to other apps.

Pick one, or ask for a mix of two. Nothing is installed to bokar83/djeli-app yet — that happens in a follow-up pass once a direction is chosen. Bottom section flags three other tools with icon problems (calculatorz.tools broken placeholders, Baobab missing maskable purpose, Money Map/Content Board with no manifest at all).

Direction A

The Kora Arc

The kora's neck, drawn as one confident sweep, cradling the resonator gourd. Two shapes, nothing else — the same restraint that makes a griot's instrument recognizable across a room. It reads as motion and instrument at once: a line being played, a story being carried up and out.

Direction B

The Griot's Voice

Three unequal arcs rising from a single point — a voice carrying, not a signal broadcasting. The asymmetry is deliberate: real speech doesn't radiate in even rings, it rises and travels in one direction. This is the most literal match to what a teleprompter actually does — it holds the words so the voice can carry.

Direction C

The Djeli Mark

A bold D built from two nested curves instead of one plain stroke — the doubled line is the idea: one story told, then carried and retold, the way a griot's lineage passes a history down. It is the most conventional app-icon shape of the three — a letterform — but the double curve keeps it from reading as generic type.
