What Was Broken (with receipts)

  1. It lied by omission. inbox_catcher.py ships a _null_fetch adapter that returns nothing; section 4 printed an all-clear it never earned, daily, since 07-10. Meanwhile cw_inbox_daily.py wrote a 48-item digest to disk at 01:00 every morning. The brief never read it. The fix is structural: a section may say "none" only after a positive fresh read. An unwired source is DOWN, not ok.
  2. It buried the one signal it did have. Section 5 was 10.7KB, duplicated (the same SW-send item appears 3 times), and dominated by items stale since 07-16. Devin was in there and you could not see him.
  3. It was 17,784 bytes against a reader contract of one phone screen. Your own standing rule for every agent is TL;DR first, bullets, recommendation first. The brief was exempting itself.
  4. It ranked cold above warm. 5 cold strangers with 4KB of drafts led; the people who already wrote to you were nowhere. Money is closer to a reply than to a first touch.

The Anti-Theatre Invariant (testable)

Every rendered line must either (a) name a person or a dated deadline plus one action you can complete today, or (b) be one of the fixed scoreboard, source-health, or blind-spot lines.

A line FAILS if it reports agent activity, counts work done, restates known state without a decision attached, or appears twice in the same brief.

Reviewer procedure: for each line ask: "If Boubacar does exactly this and nothing else today, does money move toward the $3k goal, or does a named loss get prevented?" One failing line fails the brief.

Machine proxies (in the test suite): Telegram ≤ 900 chars and ≤ 15 lines; email core ≤ 60 lines; exactly one DO THIS FIRST; zero duplicate items; zero "none" lines without a logged fresh source read; items stale > 14 days appear only inside DECIDE: KEEP OR KILL, max 3 per day.

The PRD (Constraint-First Build Spec)

Full contract at orchestrator/cos_office/PRD.md, branch docs/cos-brief-v2-prd-2026-08-04, registered in the PRD registry. Highlights below; the veto list comes before the build list on purpose.

What will NOT be built

the veto list, decided first
  1. No auto-replies or auto-sends to anyone. The CoS surfaces and recommends; you send. Structurally unchanged.
  2. No bokar83@gmail.com integration. That inbox stays per-use only. The brief names it as a blind spot (PGA mail lives there) instead of pretending coverage.
  3. No public daily-brief page. The brief names prospects and money. It travels by your own email and Telegram only.
  4. No second CRM. No per-deal valuations, no forecasting, no sentiment scores. The Money Map stays the single money source of truth.
  5. No calendar integration in v1. Verbal commitments enter via your one-line reply, not calendar scraping.
  6. No LLM-composed brief. Structure, ranking, caps, and every number are deterministic code. An LLM may only compress inbox items into one-line suggested moves, with a deterministic fallback; OpenRouter down changes nothing but polish.
  7. No new services, schedulers, or repos. Same wake, same container, same package.

The Build Contract (v1, locked)

what ships, nothing more
  1. New renderer producing the 900-char Telegram layer and the 60-line email brief from one Brief object.
  2. Section order: DO THIS FIRST (one item, why included, paste-ready draft in the email) → ABOUT TO BE LOST (≤5 deadlines inside 7 days) → YOU OWE THEM (≤7, from the inbox digest) → THEY OWE YOU (nudge-or-drop recommendation each) → MONEY (4 fixed lines incl. "yesterday: surfaced X, observed done / not done / can't tell") → DECIDE: KEEP OR KILL (max 3 stale items, rotating) → CAN'T SEE (standing) → SOURCE HEALTH (one line when green).
  3. Inbox digest adapter reading the 01:00 digest JSON (path moved into the data dir; symlink left behind), 26h freshness contract, DOWN when stale. The null-fetch section is deleted.
  4. Deterministic ranking: starred > time-sensitive > waiting > age. LLM compress pass optional.
  5. Commitments table (7 columns, frozen) written by parsing your emailed replies: log: <thing> by <date>, done <id>, kill <id>. Unparsed replies are surfaced verbatim next morning, never dropped. Dollar items route to the Money Map through its existing single writer.
  6. Escalations rewrite: dedupe, 14-day staleness router, one-time backlog amnesty triage at rollout.
  7. ASK PACK appendix in the email only: the 5 cold drafts below a cut line, capped, never on Telegram.
  8. Tests for every cap and every honesty rule, including "brief renders correctly with the LLM adapter raising".

Failure modes + success signal

how it breaks, how we know it works
  • Stale digest → SOURCE DOWN line, section renders nothing else. A missing morning beats a wrong one.
  • Wrong DO THIS FIRST → the why is on the same line, so a bad pick is visible in 2 seconds; your "kill" reply demotes it.
  • Nagging resolved items → kill-list is permanent. Nothing you said is done ever re-renders.
  • Truncation → budget allocated top-down; whole trailing sections drop, never mid-line, always with "+N more in email".

Success: caps hold 7/7 days (machine-checked); in the first 14 days, zero incidents of you surfacing an owed follow-up the sources could have shown; at least one log: reply captured.
