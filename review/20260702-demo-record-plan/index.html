Just hit record

The whole setup, no production
  • Phone or laptop camera at eye level. One window with decent light in front of you, not behind.
  • Screen share or screen recording on, with each demo's "on screen" item already open in a tab before you start.
  • Have your business brain file loaded and one real decision typed in (feeds Demos 1 and 3).
  • Say the intro line, do the thing, say the payoff line, stop. One clean take per demo. If you fumble, restart that one, do not fix it in edit.
  • Keep each under the length shown. Shorter and real beats longer and polished.
  • Save all five raw. Trim the front-door one (Demo 1) first for the workshop funnel; the rest drip as-is.
