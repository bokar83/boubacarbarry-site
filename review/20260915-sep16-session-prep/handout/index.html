Your Business Brain

Tools change. Principles don't.

You built this in the hour, on your own machine, in your own words. It works in whichever AI tool you pick up, today's or next year's. Fill it in once, paste it in once, and every answer after that comes back about your business instead of a business that does not exist.

Before anything else

Where it lives

If you did this in the session, skip ahead. Starting cold takes about two minutes.

  1. Go to claude.ai and sign in, or make a free account.
  2. Make a new Project and name it after your business. A project is a folder for one job, and it remembers what you put in it, so you are not re-explaining your business at the start of every new chat.

Use a different AI tool already? Everything below still works. Paste the brain at the top of a new chat instead of into a project.

Step 1

Fill it in

Short answers. Nobody is grading this.

What your business isPlain words, the way you would say it to a neighbor.Example: a two truck heating and air company in the south valley
What you sellYour actual jobs, services, or products.Example: repairs, tune ups, and full system replacements
Who buys itThe customer who pays you, not the one you wish you had.Example: homeowners, plus a few property managers
The problem you solve for themWhat is going wrong in their life when they call you.Example: their heat quit and they need somebody today
Your prices and packagesRough numbers are fine. This is what stops it from inventing prices.Example: service call 89, tune up 149, replacements 6k to 12k
How you talk to customersYour tone. Straight and plain, warm and chatty, formal.Example: straight, friendly, no sales pressure
Things you say a lot, and things you never sayThis is what keeps it from sounding like a stranger wrote it.Example: I say we will take care of it. I never say cutting edge.
What you repeat every single weekThe jobs that come back around whether you like it or not. These are the first things worth handing off.Example: writing quotes, chasing invoices, next week's schedule
Your biggest goal right nowThe one for this year, not the five year one.Example: add a second crew without working more Saturdays

Keep customer names, account numbers and anything private out of it. The brain describes how your business works, not the people in it.

Step 2

Paste it in once

Open your project and find its instructions. Paste this line first, then everything you wrote under it.

Everything below is about my business. Use it in every answer you give me. When a question needs a fact that is not here, ask me instead of guessing.

If the menu names moved, look for Instructions on the project page. That is the one that matters.

Step 3

Prove it worked

Ask the same thing twice. Once in a brand new chat outside the project, then once inside it.

Write a short reply to a customer who says our price is too high.

The first answer is written for a business that does not exist. The second one knows your prices and sounds like you. That gap is the whole point.

Step 4

Put it to work this week

Two jobs you already do. Inside your project, fill in the brackets and send.

The follow-up email

For when you have been staring at the words "just following up" for ten minutes.

Write a short follow-up email to [who they are]. We last talked [when and where]. What I want to happen next: [the next step, like a 15 minute call]. Keep it under 100 words and in my voice.

The review request

For when you never remember to ask, and it comes out robotic when you do.

Write a short text asking [customer first name] for a Google review. What I did for them: [what you did]. My review link: [paste link]. Warm, under 50 words.

A human approves. The AI drafts. Read every word before it goes to a customer. It is your name on it.

Step 5

Keep it alive

A brain you never open again describes a business you stopped running three months ago. Put fifteen minutes on your calendar for the first Friday of next month and read it. Add what changed and what you tried and dropped. That is the whole maintenance job.

Want it to follow you into every chat, not just the project? The three steps are on catalystworks.consulting/kit, under "Make it follow you everywhere." Do them on a laptop.
