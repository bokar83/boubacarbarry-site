The fit case: what a direct-fit search would miss

This is not a straight-line match, and pretending otherwise loses to any candidate with a real product resume. The case has to be built on the seams, not on the skills list.

The frame: they are not hiring a builder, they are hiring an owner of the whole

Read the request again. Conductor. Wear many hats. Resourceful. Twelve to fifteen people spread across a wearable, a handheld device, mobile apps, an AI layer and a practitioner portal. A company that size does not fail because the firmware is bad. It fails because five good pieces never become one product, and nobody has the standing to make the specialists converge. Every failure mode in that sentence is an organizational failure wearing a technical costume. That is the job, and that is your career.

The three you need answered before you would ever accept

  1. Do any of the devices carry FDA clearance, and how does the company describe its claims to regulators? (Their own historic marketing has claimed IP "approved by the FDA." Approval, clearance and registration are legally different things.)

  3. The category has published criticism, including an upheld UK advertising complaint under the prior name. How does the leadership team talk about that internally, and what does the evidence roadmap look like?

Ask the third one calmly and without apology. If the answer is defensive or dismissive, that tells you what you need. If the answer is specific and unbothered, that tells you something better.
