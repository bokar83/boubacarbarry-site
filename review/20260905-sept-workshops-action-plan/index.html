4. Promotion mechanics: how the pieces fit

Four channels, running at once, doing different jobs

  1. The 44 posts. Written, scored, dated. They run September 5 to 24 and are the public drumbeat. They cost you an approval and nothing else. Blocked on one word from you.
  2. The twenty messages. Private, by hand, this week. This is the channel with the actual track record.
  3. The newsletter, 46 people. The warmest list you own and the one currently doing nothing for either date. Fix it in the Sep 7 issue.
  4. Paid, $300 on Meta. Deliberately last, deliberately all on the 24th, deliberately starting the 8th so the organic arc gets three clean days first.

The Meta decision, now that the room is not a factor

Two things were sitting in front of you on the paid buy. One is gone.

Closed: whether paid traffic could point at a page whose banner said the room was being confirmed. There is no such banner. This is not deferred, it is answered.

Still yours, by Sunday the 7th: the $300 itself. The honest case for spending it is narrow and worth stating plainly — every campaign this account has run optimized for traffic or landing page views. A Purchase-optimized ad set against a paid seat has never run once. That is the untested variable, the tracking to run it is live and verified, and $300 is what it costs to find out. The honest case against is that $835 has bought one paid seat and the twenty messages are free.

Recommendation: spend it, all on the 24th, launch the 8th, hard cap $500. Not because paid is working, but because you are nineteen days from a room and this is the one lever nobody has ever pulled. If the twenty messages fill the room first, kill the spend and keep the money.

How private and public run side by side without colliding

  • The public posts never mention seats, counts, or scarcity, so a gifted seat never contradicts a post.
  • The two arcs stay separate on purpose. Online posts never mention the 24th; in-person posts never mention the 16th. Keep it that way — a person deciding between two things usually picks neither.
  • A private message always names one date, never both. Sandy drive equals the 24th. Anyone else gets the 16th.
