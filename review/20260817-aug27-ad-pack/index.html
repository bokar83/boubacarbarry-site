8. Budget and kill rules
SettingValue
Daily budget$30, already set on the ad set, all three ads share it
Run lengthAug 17 through Aug 26 at 10pm Mountain
Planned totalAbout $300
ObjectiveTraffic, optimized for landing page views. Not purchases
Floor worth running$150. Below that, fewer than 250 people reach the page and any result is indistinguishable from luck
Ceiling$40 a day. Past that you are buying repeat views inside a 25 mile ring, not new people

Kill rules, written as rules

  • Kill any single ad once it has spent $40 with a cost per landing page view above $1.20.
  • Check the placement breakdown on day two. If anything outside Facebook is taking budget, exclude it.
  • If $300 gets spent and no seat has sold, stop. That result says the page and the offer are the constraint, and more traffic will not fix it.

Why landing page views and not purchases: Meta needs roughly fifty conversions a week before an ad set stops guessing. At $50 a seat you will not produce fifty purchases in ten days, so a purchase-optimized ad set stays stuck in its learning phase and delivery stays expensive and erratic the whole way through.

Paid is the last of the four ways to reach people, behind direct asks, your own posting, and outreach. Run the ad, it is built and cheap. Do not let it become the reason the asks do not happen.
