REAL HABIT — would show up with 20 people watching too

  • Four minutes of bio before stating the concrete deliverable (00:00–00:03:59).
  • Saying essentially the same "confusion and overwhelm" positioning line twice, once at 00:03:35 and again almost word-for-word at 00:36:05, on a re-watch this reads as the tape catching a rehearsed line rather than a live thought.

The single highest-leverage change for next time

Stop refilling silence with the same sentence. When you're waiting on the room (real or imagined), say the reassurance once, then either let the pause actually sit or move immediately to something new — a second example, a quick tip, anything that isn't a restatement. This single habit is what turns the 9:01–10:51 stretch into dead air, and it's very likely the same instinct that pushes the opening bio out to 4 minutes: filling time by adding more words instead of trusting a shorter, sharper version to carry. Fix the "one thing, said once, then move" instinct and both problems shrink together.
