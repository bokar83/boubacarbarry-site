No-Name Rule: Confirmed Honored, Not Just In Theory

How it works ›

sequence_engine._extract_first_name() scores every lead's name with a confidence heuristic (real first name found = high, name looks like a business/company/unknown = low). Every one of the 5 SW templates checks first_name_confidence == "high" before rendering "Hi {name}," — on low, the greeting line is dropped entirely and the email opens straight into the hook. No template ever falls back to a broken "Hi ," or a generic "Hi there,".
