Three settings to change the same sitting

▾

The paste fixes what a person sees. These three fix what the platform believes. Do them in the same session, before you close the tab.

Turn off "Open to Work," every variant.

Including the recruiter-only version. This is the loudest job-seeker signal you are sending, and it tells the algorithm to distribute your work as a candidate's, not a consultant's.

Stop applying to jobs from this account.

Applications ran into 2025 and saved jobs into 2026. The classifier reads every one, and the job-seeker read only decays once they stop. If a backstop job search is ever needed, run it off-platform.

Set your location to your Utah metro.

Not "United States." Your new headline names Utah as the wedge; the location field should agree with it.

Flip these, then verify every change from a logged-out phone. The profile a stranger sees is the only one that counts.
