About Us

A place where kids
actually like the dentist.

We built The Point around one belief: a child's first dental experiences shape how they feel about their health for life. So we make every appointment feel unhurried, safe, and genuinely good — not just tolerable. Dr. Austin and Dr. Tew both chose pediatric dentistry because they love this age group. It shows in every visit.
