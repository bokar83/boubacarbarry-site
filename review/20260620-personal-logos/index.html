Five marks for Boubacar Barry

Five distinct symbol marks, each built to mean something and each legible as a 32×32 favicon. No initials, no monogram. A symbol with a story behind it.

Every concept ships as a full logo and a favicon crop, in an opaque navy version and a transparent -noBG version. Palette matches the site: navy, teal, amber, clay. Pick a direction (or mix and match) and it gets refined to final.

Concept 01

The Lamp

Light in the dark. Build anyway.

I learned to study by lamplight when the power went out. The flame inside the ring is the habit that built everything: you keep the light on and you keep building, whatever the grid is doing.
