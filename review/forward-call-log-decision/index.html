Forward-Call Log: public or private

Ghostwriting (PGA) positioning · not Bo McCoy · 2026-09-15

TL;DR

This is about your ghostwriting pitch (PGA), nothing to do with Bo McCoy. Part of your positioning is that you call fintech/AI trends 2-3 months before they happen. That claim only proves itself with a dated, timestamped record. This decides where that record lives — public (a weekly post) or private (a note only you see).

Recommendation: public, one short post a week. A private log proves nothing to a buyer; a public one becomes the proof itself, call by call.

What this is

You told your ghostwriting coach Devin what your approach actually is, in your own words:

"I want to focus on helping these fintech founders stay relevant... I also look at things that are set today to see where we're going in the future and be able to call that out two or three months ahead of time. When those things do happen, people come back and say, 'Oh my gosh so-and-so,' ... it happened just as he or she said."

That's three stacked things: relevance (the outcome you sell), noise filtering (telling a founder what NOT to post), and forward calling (naming where things go 2-3 months out, on the record, before they happen). Forward calling is the differentiator — and it is the one that needs proof over time, because it's a claim that either comes true in public or doesn't.

The "forward-call log" is simply that dated record: a running, timestamped list of the calls you make, so a prospective client (or Devin, or anyone) can check whether the calls actually landed.

The two options

Public recommended

One short dated post a week (LinkedIn or wherever you already post), naming a call before it lands. Consequence: the timestamp is provable by anyone, which is the only version of this claim that actually sells. Downside: you can also be publicly wrong — but a wrong call, dated and owned, still reads as someone thinking ahead in the open, which is rare on its own.

Private

A dated note only you (and maybe an agent) can see — a doc, a spreadsheet row, a private log. Consequence: the dates still count as a personal record, but nobody outside can verify them without you showing your own notes after the fact, which is a much weaker version of the same proof. It's the safer, slower option.

Recommendation

Public, one short post a week. The whole value of "forward calling" as a sales point is that it's falsifiable in public. A log nobody can see is not different, from a buyer's perspective, than no log at all. The cost is tiny (one short post a week) and it compounds: ten weeks in, you have ten dated calls and a real hit rate to point to instead of a claim you have to make about yourself.
