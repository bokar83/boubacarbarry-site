Likely pain, and why I fit

An owner at this stage is the bottleneck in their own business. The work that eats their week is follow-up, research, and admin: exactly the jobs a system can carry. They do not need more software. They need fewer hours going to repeatable work.

1Hours lost to follow-up and chasing that a system could run on its own.
2Research and prep done by hand, one lead or one task at a time.
3No time to build the leverage they know they need, because they are busy delivering.

How I would open

Suggested first line

"You mentioned doing most of it yourself. I build systems that take the repeatable parts off an owner's plate, the follow-up, the research, the admin, so you get the hours back. Worth a short look at where yours go?"

The opener references the specific thing they said, not a template, and leads with the hour they would get back, not with the tool.
