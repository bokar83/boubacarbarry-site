02

60-second call flow

A skeleton, not a script. Skip steps that don't fit the actual conversation.

› Call flow skeleton
1
Opener~10 sec

Warm, genuine, no agenda in your voice yet.

"Hey [name], it's Boubacar! How are you? Honestly you've been on my mind, wanted to actually call instead of just texting."
2
Genuine catch-up~15 sec

Ask a real question and actually listen. This is the point of the call, not a formality before the pitch.

"What's going on with you these days? How's [specific thing you know about them] going?"
3
"Here's what I'm up to"~15 sec

Drop in the 1-sentence positioning line. Say it plainly, not like a pitch.

"Yeah that's great to hear. So a quick update on my end — I spent years inside corporate HR watching good people get burned by tools that overpromised, and now I build simple AI tools for small business owners and show them exactly where AI pays off."
4
Listen / ask about them~10 sec

Turn it back to them. Let them self-select into curiosity, don't push.

"What about you, anyone in your world dealing with that kind of stuff?" or "Does that land at all with what you're seeing at [their company]?"
5
Soft next step~10 sec

Pick ONE, whichever fits how the call actually went. Referral ask ONLY if it comes up naturally.

"Would love to grab coffee sometime and catch up more properly." · "If you're ever curious, happy to just show you what I'm building, no pressure." · "If anyone comes to mind who might be curious, I'd love an intro, genuinely no pressure, just wanted you to know what I'm up to."
› Bridge lines (2–3 natural openers for after time apart)
1. "Hey, it's been way too long, been thinking about you and wanted to actually pick up the phone instead of just liking a post." 2. "I know this is a bit out of the blue, but you crossed my mind and I didn't want to just send a text." 3. (Startfest-specific) "We met at Startfest and I've been meaning to close the loop properly instead of leaving it at a LinkedIn connect."
