Everyone keeps telling small business owners the same thing about AI: use it to write your emails faster. That is the least valuable thing it can do for you. I am building a workshop right now, and the whole session is built around the opposite idea. Owner-operators bring one real decision they are stuck on. A hiring call, a pricing change, whether to drop a client. Then we run that decision through an AI setup that argues against it from every side, the way a sharp board of advisors would. You do not leave with a faster email. You leave with a decision that already survived its own worst objections. Because the thing you actually sell, the thing no tool replaces, is judgment. AI is not here to make the call for you. It is here to make sure you looked at the call from angles you would have skipped on your own. I am figuring this out in the open as I build it. If you came to a session like that, what decision would you bring?
