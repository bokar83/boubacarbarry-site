The system (open when you want the why)

▸First dollar, ranked

Every lane scored on one question: how fast and how certain is the first real dollar? The $3,000 block goal is built from these ranges.

Website audit — #1 most likely
$250–$750
Free scan first, then the $250 walkthrough on the leakers. Link live in Stripe, no fulfillment risk. The scan report is the proof, delivered same day. 4 to 6 of these is the backbone of the block goal.
Ghostwriting deposit — #2
~$0–$1,500+
First premium client, fintech founders, remote. 1 if warm asks start now. Deposit this block, full close next. Separate audience, never mixed with local.
Consulting founding client — #3
~$0–$750
$1,500 setup, $750 deposit at signature, $397/mo. ~1 close per 100 warm reach-outs. A deposit plus the start of recurring revenue toward the $12k/mo floor.
Workshop seats — not real cash
break-even
20 x $47 ~ $940, eaten by venue + ads. The money is the in-room close, and that lands in Weeks 5 to 7. Run it for proof.

Upwork is a separate hands-off lane, not counted here. Faceless = downstream, not block cash. The $12k/mo floor is the year target from the consulting self-analysis, not this block.

How they help each other

  • Shared local authority. One Google profile, one Wikidata entity, one podcast run lifts consulting, audit, and your name at once.
  • Shared content. One video becomes a LinkedIn post, an X thread, and a newsletter blurb. Repurpose once, distribute everywhere (except the faceless cluster, which stays separate).
  • Shared warm list, asked once. Each person gets one lane, one ask. Priority order resolves ties: workshop, then audit, then ghostwriting.
  • Shared proof. The July 30 recording + room photos + testimonials become the first proof asset for every brand.

The firewalls (do not cross)

Hard separations
  • Ghostwriting (fintech, remote) never mixes with the Utah-local list.
  • Faceless channels never link to your name. Separate email, separate calendar.
  • Audit and consulting must not read as rivals to the same owner. Audit feeds consulting; one person, one lane.
  • Build-in-public never pitches consulting. Personal posts always manual, no religion or politics, your voice.

▸The flywheel + the ad gate

June failed because ads ran before any proof or warm ask. This is the order that breaks the circle.

  1. Borrow trusted rooms. Free talks and co-hosts hand you an audience. Highest leverage.
  2. Send direct warm asks. The ranked list, one ask each, one lane each.
  3. Get listed everywhere a local owner looks. Silicon Slopes, chambers, Meetup, Alignable, Nextdoor, Eventbrite.
  4. Run the event, capture proof. Photos, testimonials, the recording. The first proof asset.
  5. Then run ads. Ads scale what works. Off until 10+ warm captures exist.
Ad gateMeta ads stay off until 10+ warm captures. $30/day, 3 creatives, 72h test, double the winner. Kill at $150 / 0 captures.

▸Proof, reviews, and follow-up hygiene (easy to forget)

The things that compound quietly. Adding these because the June workshop had none of them and it cost the whole event.

  • Proof capture at the workshop. Assign someone to shoot full-room photos and 2 to 3 short testimonial clips. Record the session. The recording is the single most valuable asset you walk out with.
  • Google reviews cadence. Once the profile is live, ask for one review a week (Thursday reminder). Target 15+ by block end. Reviews drive local AI answers.
  • Content repurposing. One video becomes a LinkedIn post, an X thread, a newsletter blurb, and a faceless clip. Film once, cut many.
  • Follow-up hygiene. Every warm thread gets a second and third touch (new value, one ask). Field standard for cold conversion is about 8 touches. Most money is left in the follow-up.
  • Event calendar to keep filled. Chambers, Silicon Slopes meetups, SCORE workshops. Be in one room a week so the local face builds while the funnels run.
  • List hygiene. Keep the warm list current. Quarterly value drop to dormant names, one observation, one link, no ask.
