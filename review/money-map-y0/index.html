▸The paid-ads playbook (AP's method)

This is the Meta-ads method Akshay Patel (AP) shared directly. It replaces guesswork with a tight, location-first setup built for a local workshop. Run it only after the ad gate is met (10+ warm captures), then let it scale what already works.

The minimum stack first

  • A landing page + a Stripe account. Nothing else is needed to start. The page is where the ad sends people and where the pixel lives.
  • Install a Meta pixel and embed it in the landing page. Without the pixel the algorithm is blind and cannot optimize or retarget.

How AP structures the campaign

  • Budget at the ad-set level, not the campaign level. This keeps control where the testing happens.
  • Do not fill out interest targeting. Leave it empty on purpose.
  • Use location-based targeting only: roughly a 5-mile radius around the target area (Sandy / the venue). The location is the whole audience definition.
  • Separate ad sets by format: static images in one ad set, videos in a separate ad set. 3 to 4 creatives per ad set.
The key shift: the graphic does the targetingThe way the Meta algorithm works now, the image is what finds the audience, not the interest checkboxes. So call out WHO you are looking for and their PAIN POINT directly in the ad graphic. The graphic selects the audience. That is why graphics are the priority and take the longest to get right.

How to research and recreate the graphics

  • Meta Ads Library: find the ads big influencers are running and model them. The library shows you what is already live and working.
  • Instagram search: search "ai workshops" or "workshops", scroll, and screenshot the ones that clearly work.
  • Extract the pattern: feed the screenshots to Claude or ChatGPT, pull out the common themes, then recreate in your own brand. Model the structure, not the copy.
  • On video: avoid video unless you can actually produce it. The existing X video would work as an ad if you want one in the video ad set.

▸Proof, reviews, and follow-up hygiene (easy to forget)

The things that compound quietly. Adding these because the June workshop had none of them and it cost the whole event.

  • Proof capture at the workshop. Assign someone to shoot full-room photos and 2 to 3 short testimonial clips. Record the session. The recording is the single most valuable asset you walk out with.
  • Google reviews cadence. Once the profile is live, ask for one review a week (Thursday reminder). Target 15+ by block end. Reviews drive local AI answers.
  • Content repurposing. One video becomes a LinkedIn post, an X thread, a newsletter blurb, and a faceless clip. Film once, cut many.
  • Follow-up hygiene. Every warm thread gets a second and third touch (new value, one ask). Field standard for cold conversion is about 8 touches. Most money is left in the follow-up.
  • Event calendar to keep filled. Chambers, Silicon Slopes meetups, SCORE workshops. Be in one room a week so the local face builds while the funnels run.
  • List hygiene. Keep the warm list current. Quarterly value drop to dormant names, one observation, one link, no ask.
