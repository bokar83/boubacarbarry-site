3. The one channel

Named channel: LinkedIn, in-platform, by hand.

One number eliminates every alternative: only 2.2% of your connections expose an email address. Any email-first plan aimed at your own network structurally fails. The motion has to happen inside LinkedIn.

The audience is already there and has never been pointed anywhere: 4,511 connections, 1,411 decision-makers (31.4%), 524 founders, CEOs or owners.

Constraint, stated honestly: the raw connections export was lost and re-requested, and no re-ranked list exists. Do not wait for it. You filter by title inside LinkedIn's own interface, which is where the message gets sent anyway.

4. The weekly cadence

One habit, not four.

Monday, 15 minutes, needs nobody's approval: create a LinkedIn Event for the September 24 session. It is in-platform, free, reaches the core at zero marginal cost, and leaves behind an attendee list LinkedIn maintains for you. This exact action was identified on 2026-07-23, due 2026-08-11, and never done.

Then, every weekday: five hand-sent LinkedIn messages to named owners and decision-makers. Not fifty. Five.

Why five and not ten: your demonstrated sustained rate is 33 comments in 4 days, about 8 a day. A message costs more per unit than a comment, so five is the grounded number, not a round one.

Why this sits well with how you already work: your own stated priority order puts messages and calls above commenting on other people's posts, and both above your own posts. This motion is at priority three. It is not competing with the work you already rank highest.

How the blank page problem gets solved: agents draft all five messages overnight from each person's recent activity. You edit and send. You are never staring at an empty box.

The measure for weeks one and two is messages sent, not replies received. Judging a new habit on a lagging number is how the habit dies in week two.

The existing commenting motion continues unchanged, and another lane already owns adding a connection request to each comment. Not duplicated here.
