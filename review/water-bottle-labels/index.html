How it is printed at home

Real bottle dimensions (looked up, not guessed)

The Costco Kirkland 16.9 oz / 500 ml bottle wraps at about 8.125 in circumference over an 8 in tall bottle (BottleYourBrand). The off-the-shelf Kirkland label template is 8.1875 x 1.375 in (OnlineLabels OL435). This label is 8.5 x 1.5 in: the circumference plus about 0.375 in of white overhang split across both ends so the wrap overlaps white-on-white.

The seam rule (so the tape never lands on the QR or text)

  • Leave a blank white margin on the left and right ends. The wrap overlaps white-on-white there.
  • Inset the QR and all text away from both ends so nothing important sits under the overlap or the tape.
  • Close the seam over that white margin only. The QR stays flat, clean, and scannable.

The sleeper problem: a cold bottle sweats

Straight from the cooler, the bottle is covered in water within minutes. Plain paper wicks it, inkjet ink runs, the paper warps and the label lifts. This has to be solved on purpose.

The fix (cheapest reliable path)

White paper plus a clear packing-tape laminate. Print on ordinary white paper, let it dry, then lay clear shipping tape over the whole face and burnish it flat. The tape seals the ink and gives a waterproof skin; sweat beads on it and wipes off. A few dollars of tape does dozens of bottles.

Ink economy

White is the page. No dark fills, no banners. One display-serif hero line in navy, thin teal line art, one orange accent, and the small QR. Under 10% ink coverage, so it prints cheap on a home printer.
