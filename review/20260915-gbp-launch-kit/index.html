7. Verification: what Google will ask, and what to have ready

Expect video verification. Since July 2026 this is the default for most small businesses, and it is effectively always the path for a service-area business working from home. Postcards are largely gone. You may be offered phone or email instead, and if you are, take it, it is faster.

The rules, which are strict and worth reading before you press record:

  • Recorded live on your phone, in the Google interface, while signed into the account that manages the profile. A pre-recorded file will not upload.
  • Unedited and continuous. One take, no cuts.
  • At least 30 seconds. Aim for 60 to 90. Shorter videos upload more reliably than long ones.
  • No people, no voices, no background conversation. Film when the house is quiet. This one catches people out.
  • No bank details, client documents, tax IDs, or anyone else's face in frame.

What to film, given there is no storefront and no signage. The goal is to show three things: that the business exists, that it operates, and that you are the one who runs it. In one continuous shot:

  1. Your working setup. The desk, the screens, the actual place the work happens. Let the camera settle, do not sweep it around.
  2. Proof the business is real and is yours. Show something on screen with the business name on it: the website at catalystworks.consulting, an invoice, a piece of branded material. Keep it on screen for several seconds so the reviewer can read it.
  3. Proof you are the one managing it. Without cutting, show yourself signed into a business account, or something with your name and the business name together.
  4. Close on the outside of the building, if you can do it without stopping the recording. That gives Google the location signal it is looking for, without publishing anything.

Expected wait: up to five business days. Sometimes it clears within minutes when the automated pass is confident. Most go to human review.

If it is rejected, do not resubmit repeatedly. Reinstatement takes days to weeks and repeat submissions push you back in the queue rather than forward. Tell me and we work out what the video was missing before trying again.
