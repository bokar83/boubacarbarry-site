The 7 tracks, fastest first

A. Federal small-business set-asides, $15K to $350K ONCE SAM IS LIVE (NOV-DEC)

  • Buys in this range are generally reserved for small business. You register; you don't need a certificate.
  • Below $350K there's no cap on subcontracting (13 CFR 125.6(f)(1)), and reselling is allowed. "Win it and sub the parts I can't do" is legal here.
  • Above $350K: no more than 50% can go to subs that aren't small businesses in that field.
  • Work your AI system delivers counts as yours. The test is money paid to outside firms, not your hours.
  • Reality: 6 to 24 bidders on marketing set-asides. Plan on 10 to 20 bids for 2 wins.

B. Subcontracting to big federal primes OPEN NOW

  • Primes on contracts over $900K have to hit small-business subcontracting goals.
  • Your protection: help write the prime's proposal and get named in it, and the prime owes you a good-faith effort (FAR 19.704(a)(12)). A plain quote doesn't count.
  • Method: find a Sources Sought notice on SAM.gov, look up who won that kind of work on USASpending.gov, approach 2 or 3 of those primes about that ONE opportunity, sign a teaming agreement for it. Never mass-message.
  • Pitch: "a small business that makes your subcontracting goal easy and does the AI work faster." Never identity alone.
  • Live triggers: VA Financial Management Leadership Training (due Sep 28; use it for prime research); Air Force "Total Force eLearning" (due Oct 6).
