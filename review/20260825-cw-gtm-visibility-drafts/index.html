Draft 2: Wikidata entry

Honest notability assessment — read this before submitting anything

Checked directly against Wikidata's live notability policy (Wikidata:Notability) on 2026-08-25. An item is acceptable only if it meets at least one of three criteria:

  1. At least one valid sitelink to a Wikipedia (or sister-project) page. Catalyst Works has no Wikipedia article. Not met.
  2. Refers to a clearly identifiable entity described using serious and publicly available references. In practice this means independent, secondary sources beyond the business's own website — press coverage, a directory listing from a recognized body, a partnership announcement, something that is not self-published. Catalyst Works currently has zero independent press coverage. Not met.
  3. Fulfills a structural need (needed to make statements in other Wikidata items work). Nothing currently on Wikidata needs to reference Catalyst Works to function. Not met.

Verdict: Catalyst Works does not currently meet Wikidata's notability bar. A submission today has real rejection/deletion risk — Wikidata is more permissive than Wikipedia but still routinely deletes small, pre-revenue, no-press business entries as "not notable" or promotional, even when technically referenced against the business's own site. Submitting now risks a deletion flag on the record, which is worse than not submitting at all.

Recommendation: hold Wikidata for later. Revisit once at least one of these exists:

  • A piece of independent press coverage (an article, a podcast feature, a directory profile from a recognized body — not a self-submitted listing).
  • A named partnership or client relationship that is publicly announced.
  • A Wikipedia article about Boubacar personally that a Catalyst Works item could link from (unlikely near-term, same notability bar problem one level up).

In the meantime, lower-risk, already-partially-built AEO/GEO moves carry more of the same leverage without the rejection risk: the existing llms.txt and 6/6 LocalBusiness JSON-LD are already live per the source research; a completed Crunchbase profile, a fully filled-out LinkedIn Company Page, and joining the South Valley Chamber directory (already on the harvest.md roadmap) all function as the same kind of structured, independently-hosted citation Wikidata would provide, without Wikidata's notability gate.

Owner-operators and partner-led firms in Salt Lake City and along the Wasatch Front, generally between 5 and 200 employees, who are tired of vendor pitches that start with the tool instead of the problem.

A 90-minute structured diagnostic. One named constraint, in writing, delivered within 24 hours. No deck. No discovery phase. No follow-on engagement pitched on the call. $497, one-time.
