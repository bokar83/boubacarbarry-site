1. Dave Ulrich — "AI for what?"

https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn:li:activity:7500553409117302785/

Posted about 2 hours before this build. His argument: nearly every HR leader is using AI in some form, but far fewer can say what it has actually changed for their customers, investors, or communities. He frames human capability as four elements -- talent (AI times human ingenuity), organization, leadership, and the HR operating model -- and keeps returning to one plain question: AI for what?

Dave, this is the question everyone skips. I built my own AI system before I ever advised anyone else on theirs, and the pattern is always the same: people chase the tool before they name what changes for the customer, the employee, the community on the other end of it. Talent as AI times human ingenuity is the right frame. Most stop at just AI.
