Sourcing & method

Primary -- Utah DWS Firm Find (jobs.utah.gov/utwid/firmfind-home): the state's public employer directory, built from Unemployment Insurance wage records. Free, no login. Queried its live API directly (utalmis-api.jobs.utah.gov/api/v1/firmfind/industrysearch/employers) across all 134 four-digit NAICS codes under sectors 31-33, 42 and 48-49 -- 17,147 statewide records pulled with zero fetch errors, then filtered to Salt Lake / Utah / Davis / Weber counties and the two employee-size bands closest to a 10-50 target (10-19 and 20-49), which left 2,294 qualifying companies. From that pool, 150 were selected: manufacturing (NAICS 31-33) weighted highest, then wholesale trade (42), then transportation/warehousing (48-49), spread evenly across all four counties rather than defaulting to Salt Lake County alone. 101 of the 150 rows on this list are Firm Find.

Secondary -- Utah Manufacturers Association member directory (manufacturingutah.com/member-directory/): scraped all 213 active member listings (11 pages), filtered to the four target counties (63 members), then dropped members with names that are clearly non-manufacturing services (insurance, banking, legal, staffing, real estate, healthcare, education, non-profit, hospitality) and any name already matching a Firm Find row. 49 UMA-only companies made the final list -- their value is the email addresses and websites Firm Find does not provide.

Deduplication: company names normalized (uppercased, punctuation stripped, common suffixes like Inc/LLC/Corp dropped) and compared before merging, so no company appears twice.

Not pursued / dropped: chamber-of-commerce directories were checked (Salt Lake Chamber's manufacturing category returned 1 result) and treated as too thin to be worth building against. EDCUtah has no public searchable company directory.
