1. Why this plan exists

The old engine sent text describing problems to cold strangers from bought lists: 1,142 sends, 8 replies (0.7%), 0 closes. The plan inverts all three: send a built thing (live preview site) to demand-proven Utah locals (Google Maps: high reviews + outdated or missing website) from a free database (Outscraper free tier).

2. The shape: two clocks, decoupled

Warm clock — primary
Founding-client pre-sell to your full warm list. Paste-and-send personal messages, ~1 hr/wk. 4 founding slots, two at a time. Every touch carries a second-degree intro ask. Only lane with a published benchmark chain to a close (≈1 per 100 reach-outs). Proposed start: Mon 6/15.
Cold clock — calibration
Preview Sweep: agents build preview sites for Maps-sourced locals; no-link "reply 'show me'" first touch, preview built on reply within 1 business day. Min 3-touch sequence. Proposed D0: Mon 6/29 (after IC workshop). First 40 contacts are the test, not the revenue plan.

Third leg: $250 paid Site Walkthrough Audit — first rung of the cold offer ladder, purchasable from day 1 of warm asks. Backstop: operator-level P&L counts IC workshop + CW Upwork receipts at actuals, with a week-6 reallocation trigger (SW cash under $500 → half of SW hours redeploy to proven rails).

3. The honest money math

90-day SW base case: $2,400–4,900. One close lands $2,400–2,700 (under the $3–5k bar — stated plainly). Two closes land $4,300–4,900. At 150+ effective warm reach the benchmark expectation is ~1.5 closes, so the modal outcome straddles the bar rather than sitting under it. Success case $5,500–8,000 (4-slot ceiling). Spend: ~$7/mo cold-send Workspace seat, $10 preview domain, PO Box $5–30/mo. Tools $0.

4. What it costs you

~3 hrs/wk contract, front-loaded: one 35-min WARM-GO sitting (prune the warm list, one-click approve the audit link, paste-send 2 practice-call invites), then ~10–15 min paste-send blocks and inline Telegram approvals. A 55-minute LOW-POWER mode pre-defines what shrinks when a week goes sideways. Time is measured, not assumed — 2 missed weeks auto-fires a strategy review.

What the 81 does NOT cover — residual doubts, plainly

  1. Base case sits under the revenue bar and needs 2+ closes from an org with 0 lifetime closes. Only run data fixes this.
  2. No published data anywhere that preview-in-hand lifts cold replies. The 4–6% benchmark band is vendor data from warmed, practiced senders.
  3. Warm list size and your weekly time are both unverified — the two inputs the primary lane depends on. The 6/12 prune count and week-1 measured minutes are the test.
