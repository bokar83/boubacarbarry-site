Details — tap to open

1 · Why this plan exists (the autopsy)

The old engine sent text describing problems to cold strangers from bought lists: 1,142 sends, 8 replies (0.7%), 0 closes. The plan inverts all three: send a built thing (live preview site) to demand-proven Utah locals (Google Maps: high reviews + outdated or missing website) from a free database (Outscraper free tier). Activation order corrected: warm first, cold second — the cheapest leads were never worked.

2 · The shape: two clocks, decoupled
Warm clock — primary
Founding-client pre-sell to your full warm list. Paste-and-send personal messages, ~1 hr/wk. 4 founding slots, two at a time. Every touch carries a second-degree intro ask. Only lane with a published benchmark chain to a close (≈1 per 100 reach-outs). Proposed start: Mon 6/15.
Cold clock — calibration
Preview Sweep: no-link "reply 'show me'" first touch, preview built on reply within 1 business day. Min 3-touch sequence, dedicated send identity, compliance footers. Proposed D0: Mon 6/29 (after IC workshop). Warm results can switch the cold OFFER, never throttle cold volume.

Third leg: $250 paid Site Walkthrough Audit — rung 1 of the cold offer ladder, purchasable from day 1 of warm asks.

3 · The honest money math

90-day SW base case: $2,400–4,900. One close lands $2,400–2,700 (under the $3–5k bar — stated plainly). Two closes land $4,300–4,900. At 150+ effective warm reach the benchmark expectation is ~1.5 closes, so the modal outcome straddles the bar rather than sitting under it. Success case $5,500–8,000 (4-slot ceiling). Operator-level P&L adds IC workshop + CW Upwork receipts at actuals, so income-if-SW-stalls is not $0. Spend: ~$7/mo cold-send Workspace seat, $10 preview domain, PO Box $5–30/mo. Tools $0.

One 35-min WARM-GO sitting (prune the warm list, one-click approve the audit link, paste-send 2 practice-call invites), then ~10–15 min paste-send blocks and inline Telegram approvals. A 55-minute LOW-POWER mode pre-defines what shrinks when a week goes sideways. Time is measured, not assumed — 2 missed weeks auto-fires a strategy review. Five recorded practice reps before the first real founding call.

9 · Front-end pressure test (your yellow flag) — 6/10 PM

Verdict: YELLOW on cold-as-backstop confirmed. Cold email at plan volume needs ~3,300–7,700 contacts for 12 clients = 1.6–3.7 years. Cold is calibration + backstop, never the machine. Your referral-compound thesis is CORRECT: 2 closes in 90 days + referrals ≈ 4–6 clients by month 6 — which is why the warm lane and the founding window are load-bearing.

Your hypothesis validated: results-IN-the-first-email (top 3 findings as plain text, no link) is both the best-deliverability AND best-psychology first touch. The plan's cold opener is currently offer-led ("I made a concept, reply 'show me'") — it gets rewritten findings-led ("3 things on your Google listing costing you money") before any first touch. Template change, zero new infra.

3 fixes going into the plan:

  1. Watcher counts SENDS, not builds. The "no leads in 3 weeks" trap is contacts-never-sent; current spec tracks build completions, so the stall would be invisible until week 3. Minimum 1 touch/day becomes a watcher rule.
