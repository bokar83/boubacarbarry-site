Watch-outs from the Sadia session (apply live)

Direct carryover from Boubacar's own review of a prior first session. Apply these deliberately tonight, not as an afterthought.

  • Let him talk more. Resist filling silence. After a question, wait. The coaching value is in what he says, not in how much Boubacar says.
  • Draw the decision out of him, don't supply it. When he's stuck between two paths, ask what he thinks the tradeoff is before offering a take. The insight has to be his, or it won't stick.
  • Drop the rhetorical "Okay?" tag. It reads as seeking agreement rather than genuine coaching — cut it from sentence endings entirely tonight.
  • One task at the close, not three. Leave him with exactly one concrete next step, not a list.

Question bank, grouped by GROW

Goal

  • "What does success look like six months from now — paint the picture."
  • "Is this about landing any job, the right job, or something bigger you're trying to change about your path?"
  • "What would make today feel like a win when you walk away?"

Reality

  • "Walk me through where you've been — roles, industries, what's connected them."
  • "What have you already tried toward this goal, and what happened?"
  • "What's the honest, hardest part of where you're stuck right now?"
  • "Who else knows you're working on this, and how are they helping or not helping?"

Options

  • "What are the two or three different directions you could take from here?"
  • "If time and money weren't a constraint, what would you try first?"
  • "Which of these have you already ruled out, and why?"

Will

  • "Of everything we've talked about, what's the one thing you're actually going to do this week?"
  • "On a scale of 1-10, how committed are you to that right now?"
  • "What could get in the way, and what will you do about it?"

AI-in-job-search tools (offer once his situation is clear)

Match the tool to whatever stage surfaces in Reality/Options — don't lead with tools before the real problem is named.

  • Targeting unclear: paste his background into Claude or ChatGPT and ask for 10 competitive job titles plus 3-5 adjacent roles he may not have considered, with reasoning for each.
  • Low response rate: AI-assisted resume/cover-letter tailoring per role, plus warm-path research on target companies.
  • Interviews not converting: STAR-story builder and AI mock-interview prompts to turn his experience into structured, practiced answers.
  • Wants a portfolio piece: identify a real problem from his own work history, build a small working tool with AI, document it (GitHub, Loom, or a written explainer) to show in interviews.
