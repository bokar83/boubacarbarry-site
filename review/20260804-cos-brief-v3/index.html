The Running Register

Your ask: "a running track of everything that's being done and everything that's running... the other items that are supposed to be automated and automatic." Today's evidence for why exit codes cannot be trusted: the inbox digest cron exited 0 every night since mid-July while producing nothing (wrong label name), and the warm-list feed has been dead 33 days while nothing complained loudly enough to reach you.

The register rule: OK is earned, not assumed. Every registered job declares an output probe (artifact freshness + a content sanity check like a nonzero row count) and a freshness window. OK renders only from a passing probe. A failing or stale probe renders DOWN with its age, ranked as a loss-prevention item next to money items, not buried in a footer. A job with no declarable probe renders UNPROBED, an honest third state, so "we cannot verify this" can never dress up as "verified". Exit codes and log lines are never accepted as proof of life.

Blockers are exempt from age thresholds. The ranking model applies a minimum-age floor to ordinary follow-ups so fresh threads do not nag. A same-day finding showed why that floor must never apply to a BLOCKER: the SLCC date decision was written to yesterday, so an age floor would hide it tomorrow, while it is the single dependency behind the campaign window, a written promise to J., and the referral kit's date. Rule now in the contract: an item that blocks other tracked work surfaces every day until it resolves, with its dependents named; age orders items within a bucket but never decides inclusion for a blocker. Blocking is derived without any language model: a tracked item carries a "blocks:" list, and nonempty means blocker. Also folded in: buying-unit grouping now keys on the ORGANIZATION map, not on matched personal addresses (two partner addresses sharing no surname or domain are still one unit, one row, one count), and ages print as floored whole days by convention.

Every rendered item must fit exactly one bucket or it does not render; an empty bucket prints its fresh-read receipt ("No decisions were taken on your behalf yesterday: claims and task rows read at 08:31") so empty is evidence, never absence. And the anti-theatre invariant polices the first bucket: a DECIDED FOR YOU entry must be a real decision you could meaningfully override, or it is action theatre in a suit and gets cut.
