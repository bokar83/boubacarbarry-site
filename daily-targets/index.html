The short version

Every target on this page is something only Boubacar can do. A comment, a connection request, a message, a call. Nothing on the list can be handed to software, and that is deliberate.

Tomorrow, 2026-08-18: five genuine comments, five connection requests behind those comments, every thanks-and-seed message that has come due, one forward call written down, and thirty minutes of staying present after the batch goes out.

The thing this page is designed against: a ranked list of twenty contacts sat untouched for eleven days while something shipped every single day. Building is not the constraint. Conversations are.

Today's card

Five targets. Tick as you go. Nothing here needs a decision first, so it can start tomorrow morning without waiting on anything else on this page.

1 Five genuine comments on prospect posts

Devin calls this the highest-leverage item on the whole list. Comment on their posts and on their comments. Value, not congratulations. A comment that agrees is invisible; a comment that adds the thing they left out is not.

2 Connection requests, no note, after the comment

The first five are the floor, in amber. Six through ten are the stretch. Order matters and it is the whole trick: the comment lands first, so the request arrives from a name they have already read rather than a stranger. No note attached. A note turns a free yes into a decision.

3 Every thanks-for-connecting that has come due

Fires the moment an invite is accepted, not on a schedule. Check the pipeline below for anyone sitting at Connected with no message sent. This is step 3 of the sequence and it is the one that quietly decides whether any of the earlier work turns into a conversation.

4 The seeding question, to anyone already thanked

Why are they not posting more, and what is blocking it. Their answer is the whole opening for everything after, because it is the only thing on this page you cannot research in advance. Target a real conversation inside three to four messages, then stop counting messages and start listening.

5 One forward call, written down and dated

One line. What is set today, and where it goes in two or three months. This is the only target on the list that is not an ask, and it earns its place because the proof only accrues if the call is timestamped before the event. Every day it is skipped is credibility that cannot be recovered later.

Closing out writes a dated row to the database and clears the ticks and the logs for tomorrow. The dated row stays, so the history builds itself.

The week around it

The daily card is the same five things every weekday. The week adds three things the day cannot hold.

WhenWhatWhy it is not a daily item
Monday to Friday The card above. Five comments, five to ten requests, follow-ups due, one forward call, thirty-minute window. It is the daily item.
Wednesday Read the pipeline below top to bottom. Anything at Connection Sent for more than a week goes to Nurture rather than sitting. A stalled row looks identical to a live one until someone looks. Once a week is enough; every day is fiddling.
Friday Count the week. Comments, requests, replies, conversations, calls booked. Write the number down even when it is zero. A zero written down is data. A zero not written down is a story about being busy.
Sunday, the sitting Build next week's list. Devin prescribes this and it is the load-bearing hour of the week. Twenty-five to fifty names screened against the disqualifier, so Monday morning has nothing to decide. List-building and outreach compete for the same attention, and outreach loses every time. Separating them is the point.

Why Sunday matters more than it looks. Every morning that starts with "who do I talk to today" is a morning that starts with a decision, and a decision is where the day leaks. The sitting moves all of the week's deciding into one hour where it is cheap.

The forward-call log

This is the cheapest credibility asset available and it only works if it starts now. A forward call is worth something exactly because it was written down before the thing happened. Written down afterwards it is worth nothing, and no amount of later effort can fix that.

Ten weeks of one call a week is ten dated calls and a hit rate that is a number instead of a claim. A prospect who watches one call land is closer to buying than any case study would put them.

One row a day from the card above, or a longer one here when the call deserves it. Keep the date, the call, and what would prove it wrong.

One decision belongs to Boubacar and nobody else can make it: where these get published. A log kept privately proves nothing to a buyer. A log posted publicly is the differentiator working in the open, and it is also the version that can be wrong in public. That trade is his to take, and it is the single open question on this whole page.

The sequence, as Devin gave it

Five steps, in order. The order is the method, not a suggestion.

  1. Comment on their posts and on their comments. Genuine value. Not congratulations.
  2. Connection request after the comment, with no note. The comment is the note.
  3. Thanks for connecting, once accepted.
  4. Seed it: why are they not posting more, what is blocking it.
  5. Lead with the value, which is being current on the tech. Target a real conversation inside three to four messages.

The hard disqualifier, in Devin's words: if they are not posting on LinkedIn, they are not a lead. That is a filter, not a preference. It runs before anything else and it does not get argued with.

It may cut names already on the list. Cutting them is the system working.

Volume, and an honest conflict

What he said live today: five to ten connection requests a day. That is the number in force and it is what the card above implements.

Devin's written outreach engine document is reported to say twenty-five a day full time and ten part time. Both numbers are written here on purpose, so that nobody silently reverts to the higher one later and calls it the plan. The live instruction wins.

Stated plainly: that written document is not in any repository I can read. The twenty-five and ten figures come from the brief I was given, not from a file I opened. The five to ten is what he was told live today and is the number to run.

What is deliberately not on this page

No comment drafts. No message drafts. No seeding lines. The entire method is specificity to one person, and a template is the fastest way to destroy it. What is here is the shape and the count. The words are his, every time, written in front of the actual post.

Where the ranking still holds, and where it does not

The reachability argument for putting the faucets first survives the disqualifier untouched, because publication operators post by definition. The founder ranking does not survive it: it was built on company fit and freshness, and posting activity was never a factor. Re-rank the founders by posting activity first and fit second, not the other way round.

What is deliberately not on his list

The rule this page is built on: if software could do it, it does not belong on a founder's daily targets. These are real work and they still do not qualify.

  • Finding contact routes for the twenty.
  • Assembling what a prospect has said publicly, before he writes anything.
  • Tracking replies across channels so nothing depends on memory.
  • Arming follow-ups.
  • Keeping this page and the pipeline current.
  • Drafting anything for review.

Why the list is this short on purpose. A ranked list of twenty contacts sat untouched for eleven days while something shipped every single day. The output was never the problem. Five targets that only he can do, every morning, is the correction, and the moment this page grows a sixth thing that an agent could have done, it has stopped working.
